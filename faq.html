I

Admissions & fees

Routing note: enquiries here go to Admissions.

What does it cost?

Fees are kept fair and competitive, with the full school day, recordings and pastoral care included. We share current fees on enquiry — register your interest and we'll send them with no obligation.

Which year groups can join, and when?

The Academy is opening in stages, from primary through to Sixth Form. Register your interest and tell us your child's year group and preferred start (for example September 2026 or January 2027) and we'll let you know when it opens for them.

How do we apply?

It's a calm, three-step process: register your interest online, have a conversation with us so we can understand your child's needs and answer your questions, then join as a registered family when your child's year group opens. No pressure and no countdown.

Can I register more than one child?

Yes. Register your eldest (or any one) child through the form, then use the message field to tell us about any brothers or sisters you'd like to register too — we'll follow up about each of them.

II

Curriculum & stages of school

Routing note: enquiries here go to Academic / Curriculum.

What curriculum do you teach?

The full British curriculum, thoughtfully sequenced and rigorously assessed, from primary through to A Level — planned and taught by qualified, experienced UK teachers.

What stages and ages do you cover?

Primary School (Key Stages 1–2, ages 5–11), Secondary School through to GCSE (Key Stages 3–4, ages 11–16), and Sixth Form for A Levels (Key Stage 5, ages 16–18) — one school, taught live and in proper sequence, so a pupil can stay with us all the way to university.

What subjects are taught?

Primary: English, Maths, Science, Humanities, Qur'an & Islamic Studies, Arabic and PSHE. Secondary: core and option GCSEs, Sciences, Languages and Islamic Studies. Sixth Form: A Levels with university and UCAS guidance.

Are Qur'an and Islamic Studies part of the curriculum, or an extra?

They're built into the timetable at every stage, not an add-on — taught alongside the British curriculum subjects as part of the same school day.

Are lessons live, or pre-recorded videos?

Lessons are taught live and timetabled, with pupils speaking, answering and taking part in real time — just as in a physical classroom. Every live lesson is also recorded, so it can be revisited, but the teaching itself is live.

III

The school day & technology

Routing note: enquiries here go to Academic / Pastoral, or Technical support once places are allocated.

What does a school day look like?

Mornings open with live core lessons, midday has a proper break with protected time for ṣalāh, and the afternoon continues with further lessons and guided independent work. Every lesson is recorded so pupils can catch up or revise at any time. Exact lesson times are confirmed with families before term begins.

What will my child need at home?

A computer or tablet, a reliable internet connection, and a quiet place to work. Everything else — the virtual classroom, materials and lesson recordings — is provided through the school portal.

We're not in the UK — does the timetable still work for us?

The Academy is one school, whether a family is in Manchester, Dubai or Kuala Lumpur — all that's needed is a connection. Because every lesson is recorded, a different time zone or a missed class is never a barrier.

Is there homework, and how is progress tracked?

Yes — homework is set and marked through the school portal, with regular feedback, so progress is tracked all year rather than left to one exam. There's a register, reports, and teachers who know each pupil by name.

IV

Islamic ethos & pastoral care

Routing note: enquiries here go to Pastoral / Admissions.

What is tarbiyyah, and how does it show up day to day?

Tarbiyyah — the nurturing of character and faith — is the heart of the Academy, not a timetable slot. It lives in how teachers speak to pupils, in a reflective start to the week, in the akhlaq and adab modelled and expected throughout the day, and in pastoral care from form tutors who know each child.

Is this a full school, or a weekend/supplementary programme?

A full school. Live timetabled lessons, qualified UK teachers, a register, homework, feedback and reports — the structure of a proper school, delivered to your home, and backed by an established trust.

Who is the Academy for?

Practising Muslim families in the UK and abroad seeking a values-aligned British education — including expat families and those who travel or move often. All who share our ethos are warmly welcome.

How do you keep pupils safe and supported online?

Small, deliberately limited class sizes so teachers give each pupil genuine attention, computer monitoring and clear expectations around engagement, and a pastoral service that mentors pupils and encourages safe choices.
