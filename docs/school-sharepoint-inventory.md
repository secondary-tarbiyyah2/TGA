## Years 7 to 9: order and topics against the school's 2026-27 files
- Islamic Studies: the school teaches aqeedah first (Year 7 tawheed and the Messenger; Year 8 AQ 8.01 tawheed, the
  prophets, angels, the Last Day, resurrection, al-Qadar), then fiqh (Year 7 taharah from November), then seerah in
  term 3 (Year 8; Year 9 seerah and jihad), with a hadith series all year. TGA's map has the same topics: aqeedah in
  Autumn 1 of each year, fiqh and hadith in Spring, but puts seerah in Autumn 2 of Years 7 and 8. The founding year
  starts in January, so no Autumn unit is taught in 2026-27; the seerah timing is listed to confirm for 2027-28 and
  nothing was moved.
- Maths: Year 7 starts with algebra (functions, expressions, substitution) as TGA's White Rose map does. Years 8 and 9
  start with statistics (tables, pie charts, stem and leaf; averages and surveys), from an Edexcel KS3 progression;
  TGA's Years 8 and 9 follow White Rose, as the alignment brief fixes, so the order was not changed.
- Geography: the Year 9 2026-27 folder holds Oxford Geog.3 units (escaping poverty, climate change in Yakutia), the
  series TGA's KS3 map already follows.
- English: the 2026 HT1 schemes (Y7 to Y9 LANG SOW HT1 2026) were already used in the 9 Nov alignment; the 2026-27
  presentation folders hold nothing newer for Years 7 to 9.
- Other KS3 subjects (science, art, PSHE, history, Arabic): no 2026-27 folders were found, so the 2023-24 to 2025-26
  files already used stand.

- The 2026-27 decks use em dashes throughout (titles and slides).
