Assessment OF Learning =  (Summative) 
Students are evaluated upon completion of the work and the focus is on the final product. (Exam)

Assessment FOR Learning = (Formative)
On going observations and methods of evaluation designed to measure student understanding of a concept or task in order to identify areas that require more/different teaching
