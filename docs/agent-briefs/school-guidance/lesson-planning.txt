Lesson Planning
Learning Objectives
--- slide 10
Teacher subject knowledge and demonstration – very important
Explain H&S rules. Show all the ingredients and how much to use. Display the recipe and let pupils copy enforcing reading/writing skills. Explain measurements enforcing mathematics skills.
Show a video clip on Yasir making the soup, allowing students to take notes and measure at the same time.
Visual/audio/kinaesthetic learning.
