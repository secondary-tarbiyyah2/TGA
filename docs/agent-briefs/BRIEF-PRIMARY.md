## Weekly lessons (TGA Curriculum Plan 2027)
- Year 2: 15 live lessons of 30 min: English 5 (Mon-Thu Little Wandle: Year 2 starts with Phase 5 review then moves to the
  Year 2 spelling programme once secure; Fri writing on the project's text type), Maths 4 (White Rose Year 2), Topic 2
  (the Cornerstones project: science, history, geography, art, DT; computing in topic once a fortnight), Qur'an with Qaidah/tajweed 3,
  Islamic Studies / PSHE 1 (alternate weeks).
- Years 3-6: 20 live lessons of 45 min: English 5 (reading, writing, grammar/spelling), Maths 5 (White Rose), Science 2,
  History and geography 2 (the Cornerstones main project), Computing OR Art and DT 1 (they alternate by half term: Autumn 1, Spring 1,
  Summer 1 = computing; Autumn 2, Spring 2, Summer 2 = art and DT, using the companion art/DT projects above),
  Qur'an and Tajweed 2, Arabic 1 (Kitabi), Islamic Studies 1, PSHE 1.
Home learning: daily reading 20 min, Qur'an revision 10-15 min, weekly maths and spelling practice.

## Qur'an spine (hifdh, minimum new memorisation this year; revise all earlier surahs)
Y1 Al-Fatihah, 114-105 · Y2 104-98 + Ayat al-Kursi · Y3 97-93 · Y4 92-89 + Al-Baqarah 285-286 · Y5 88-85 · Y6 84-81.
Tajweed: Y2 Qaidah completion (tanween, madd letters, shaddah, reading from the mushaf) · Y3 noon sakinah and tanween; meem sakinah ·
Y4 heavy and light letters; idgham; families of madd · Y5 makharij; laam rules; stopping signs · Y6 sifat of the letters; reciting with beauty.
Arabic (Kitabi, as at Tarbiyyah Primary, pupils placed by level): Y3 letters, vocabulary, short sentences · Y4 the definite article,
gender, days and places · Y5 describing myself, numbers, likes · Y6 present-tense verbs, my day, questions.
Islamic Studies: Y2 stories of the Prophets; the mission in Makkah and the Hijrah; purity, prayer and good speech ·
Y3 tawheed; the Prophet ﷺ in Madinah; wudu and the adhan; intention and gratitude · Y4 Islam, Iman and Ihsan; the Makkan years
in depth; the pillars · Y5 tawheed and qadar; Ta'if to the Hijrah; zakah, sawm and Hajj; truthful speech, online too ·
Y6 the names and attributes of Allah; the pillars of iman in depth; the later Madinan years and the Prophet's ﷺ character; preparing for secondary.
