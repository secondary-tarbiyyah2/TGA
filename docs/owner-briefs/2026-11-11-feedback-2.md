## Principle that reconciles this with the 11 Nov 08:28 feedback
- Small actions (set work, add a post, log a mistake, report an absence) open as a well-sized focused dialog or side
  panel that fits the screen without scrolling the page behind.
- Big tasks (a lesson's register, a pupil's full profile, a quiz builder, an assessment) get their own focused page or
  full-screen view with a clear back button; never a long accordion that expands everything on one page.
- Nothing jumps the page downwards when a button is pressed; no "everything expanded at once" lists.
- Remove redundancy: if two places show the same thing, keep the better one.
