# Year 8: overview
## At a glance
- Key Stage 3, 23 lessons a week (50 minutes each): English 4, Maths 4, Science 3, History 1, Geography 1, Computing 1, Art and design 1, Qur'an and Tajweed 3, Arabic 2, Islamic Studies 2, PSHE 1. No PE.
- Year 8 is planned backwards from the Year 10 International GCSE courses: history, geography and English units were added to fill gaps; units marked * in the plan exist in the portal (English war poetry; Science 8A and 8E; History Industrial Revolution; Geography coasts; Computing My Digital World; Art eight-pointed star; PSHE; Arabic; Qur'an; Islamic Studies).
- Founding year (first live lessons Mon 4 Jan 2027, induction Fri 1 Jan): Spring 1 to Summer 2 only, 99 teaching days over 21 weeks. Maths and English start with a two-week bridge (4 and 11 Jan) of the Autumn essentials.
- Summer 1 has five dated teaching weeks and Summer 2 has four, so week 6 of Summer 1 and weeks 5 and 6 of Summer 2 are dropped in 2027 (marked EXTENSION in each subject).
- Qur'an: hifdh Al-Mulk to Nuh (67 to 71: الملك إلى نوح), complete noon and meem rules, when letters meet, stopping and starting.
- Online delivery: live Teams lessons, maximum 15 per class; practicals and fieldwork are done at home or locally with a written guide and a live debrief. No trips, visitors or theme weeks.
- Autumn units skipped in 2027 (Tudors and Ottomans, food and respiration, binary, star design, etc.) are picked up in the three-week review at the start of Autumn 2027.
- NEW lessons are needed for: most of Maths, Science from Spring 2, the Summer units in every subject, and Animal Farm.

## Assessment
- Baseline on entry: GL entry assessments, Qur'an recitation placement, Arabic placement and reading age, in induction week (Fri 1 Jan) and on joining later.
- Every lesson: retrieval starter and exit ticket. End of every unit (except PSHE): short quiz (15 to 20 minutes) in the portal's secure test mode.
- End of each half term: hifdh check and tajweed rubric for all Qur'an groups; Spring 1 ends 12 Feb, Spring 2 ends 23 Apr, Summer 1 ends 12 Jun (dates TO CONFIRM).
- Assessment point 1 (December): not applicable in the founding year. Assessment point 2: week of 26 Apr (core subject tests, report to parents). Assessment point 3: week of 14 Jun (end-of-year tests in every subject, report to parents in July).
- Teachers record one judgement per subject at each point (working towards, at or above the expected standard); pupils working towards get a support plan before the next point.
- Year 8 end of year bridges to Year 9; the three-week review at the start of Autumn 2027 closes gaps from skipped Autumn units.

# English
## At a glance
- 4 lessons a week. Year 8 reads war poetry (Autumn), Animal Farm (Spring) and nineteenth-century short stories with descriptive writing (Summer).
- Founding year: the two bridge weeks (4 and 11 Jan) teach the eight portal war poetry lessons, so no Autumn essentials are lost; Animal Farm then runs from 18 Jan.
- Skills thread all year: PEE/PEEL paragraphs, inference, comparing writers, vocabulary, sentence variety, a weekly spelling and punctuation starter.
- Prepares for International GCSE English Language B (4EB1) and Literature (4ET1) in Years 10 and 11.
- TO CONFIRM: text suitability for Animal Farm (animal characters are pigs; mentions of drinking) and for the chosen short stories before any text is issued.

# Islamic Studies
## At a glance
- 2 lessons a week. Year 8: the pillars of iman in depth; the road to Madinah (Seerah); the sources of Islamic law; hadith and akhlaq; Important Lessons for Every Muslim (units 2 and 3).
- The portal has 12 Year 8 lessons in four units: The Pillars of Iman in depth (3), Seerah: the road to Madinah (4), Fiqh and the sources of Islamic law (3), Hadith and Akhlaq (2). The rest are NEW.
- Never invent hadith, ayat or scholars' words: every text comes from a checked source. TO CONFIRM: the edition of Important Lessons for Every Muslim used, and which titles are units 2 and 3 in it.
- Founding year: Spring 1 teaches the Autumn units in a compressed form, because belief and the Seerah are the foundation for the law and character units.
- No images of prophets or of Allah.
