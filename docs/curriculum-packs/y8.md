# Year 8: overview
## At a glance
- Key Stage 3, 23 lessons a week (50 minutes each): English 4, Maths 4, Science 3, History 1, Geography 1, Computing 1, Art and design 1, Qur'an and Tajweed 3, Arabic 2, Islamic Studies 2, PSHE 1. No PE.
- Year 8 is planned backwards from the Year 10 International GCSE courses: history, geography and English units were added to fill gaps; units marked * in the plan exist in the portal (Science 8A and 8E; Computing My Digital World; Art eight-pointed star; PSHE; Arabic; Qur'an; Islamic Studies). English, History and Geography now follow the school's own 2026-27 schemes and intents; portal History Industrial Revolution and Geography coasts lessons are reused, and the portal English war poetry lessons now fit Year 9 conflict poetry.
- Founding year: induction week Mon 4 to Fri 8 Jan 2027 (accounts, device checks, meet teachers, entry and baseline assessments; no live lessons); first live lessons Mon 11 Jan 2027. Spring 1 to Summer 2 only, 94 teaching days over 20 weeks (Spring 1 has 5, so each subject drops one Spring 1 week). Maths and English start with a two-week bridge (11 and 18 Jan) of the Autumn essentials.
- Summer 1 has five dated teaching weeks and Summer 2 has four, so week 6 of Summer 1 and weeks 5 and 6 of Summer 2 are dropped in 2027 (marked EXTENSION in each subject).
- Qur'an: hifdh Al-Mulk to Nuh (67 to 71: الملك إلى نوح), complete noon and meem rules, when letters meet, stopping and starting.
- Online delivery: live Teams lessons, maximum 15 per class; practicals and fieldwork are done at home or locally with a written guide and a live debrief. No trips, visitors or theme weeks.
- Autumn units skipped in 2027 (Gothic short stories and Frankenstein, the Renaissance to the Civil War, fieldwork, population and urban change, food and respiration, binary, star design, etc.) are picked up in the three-week review at the start of Autumn 2027.
- NEW lessons are needed for: most of Maths, every Science unit except 8A and 8E, the Summer units in every subject, and Animal Farm.

## Assessment
- Baseline on entry: GL entry assessments, Qur'an recitation placement, Arabic placement and reading age, in induction week (Mon 4 to Fri 8 Jan) and on joining later.
- Every lesson: retrieval starter and exit ticket. End of every unit (except PSHE): short quiz (15 to 20 minutes) in the portal's secure test mode.
- End of each half term: hifdh check and tajweed rubric for all Qur'an groups; Spring 1 ends 12 Feb, Spring 2 ends 23 Apr, Summer 1 ends 12 Jun (dates TO CONFIRM).
- Assessment point 1 (December): not applicable in the founding year. Assessment point 2: week of 26 Apr (core subject tests, report to parents). Assessment point 3: week of 14 Jun (end-of-year tests in every subject, report to parents in July).
- Teachers record one judgement per subject at each point (working towards, at or above the expected standard); pupils working towards get a support plan before the next point.
- Year 8 end of year bridges to Year 9; the three-week review at the start of Autumn 2027 closes gaps from skipped Autumn units.

# English
## At a glance
- 4 lessons a week in the school's weekly sequence (Literature, Non-fiction, Language, Improvement).
- Concept: Power, Relationships and Society (how is power gained, used and abused?). Texts in order: Gothic short stories, Frankenstein, Animal Farm, Julius Caesar, poetry of voice and hope.
- Frameworks grow into comparison and rhetoric: PETZAAL, PESTAAL, TAPS, PEMAACS, GSP, PEEDL, HEMPS, CA/CR, CON.
- The school's Year 8 HT4 (Ramadan programme) file is empty: Summer 2 is a TGA persuasive writing and end-of-year unit (TO CONFIRM).
## Autumn 1: Gothic short stories: the power of fear
- Gothic conventions, atmosphere and suspense; The Landlady (Roald Dahl), The Monkey's Paw (W W Jacobs), The Tell-Tale Heart (Edgar Allan Poe); Language analysis (PETZAAL), structure analysis (PESTAAL) and comparison of writers; Grammar: sentence types for suspense; non-fiction on fear.
- Assessment: End-of-half-term assessment (week 7). Resources: Order, texts and assessments: Tarbiyyah Secondary English Language and Literature weekly scheme of work 2026-27 (Year 8 HT1, 7 weeks) and the English Intent 2026-27.
- Founding year: Not taught as a unit in 2027: PETZAAL and PESTAAL essentials in the bridge (11 and 18 Jan); the stories go to the Autumn 2027 review.

## Autumn 2: Frankenstein (Mary Shelley): creation and responsibility
- The Creature's first moments, rejection and prejudice, education, revenge; Victor's responsibility as a creator; science and its limits; Non-fiction: Mary Shelley and science fiction; should science have limits?; Grammar: colons and semi-colons, relative clauses, parenthesis, formal register.
- Assessment: End-of-half-term assessment (week 7). Resources: Tarbiyyah Secondary English Language and Literature weekly scheme of work 2026-27 (Year 8 HT2, titled 'Frankenstein (Part 2)'). TO CONFIRM: edition or adaptation used.
- Founding year: Not taught in 2027: Autumn 2027 review.
