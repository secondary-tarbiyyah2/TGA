# Year 2: overview

## At a glance

- 15 live 30-minute lessons a week on Microsoft Teams (maximum 15 pupils): English 5 (Mon to Thu Little Wandle, Fri writing), Maths 4 (White Rose Year 2), Topic 2 (the Cornerstones project: science, history, geography, art, DT; computing in lesson B once a fortnight), Qur'an with Qaidah and tajweed 3, Islamic Studies / PSHE 1 (alternate weeks: Islamic Studies in odd weeks, PSHE in even weeks of each half term).
- Projects follow Tarbiyyah Primary's Long Term Plan 2026-2027 exactly: Bounce, Wriggle & Crawl, Muck, Mess & Mixtures, Towers, Tunnels & Turrets, Street Detectives, The Scented Garden, with the companion projects named in each unit.
- Founding year: induction week Mon 4 to Fri 8 Jan 2027 (accounts, device checks, meet teachers, baseline assessments; no live lessons); first live lessons Mon 11 Jan 2027; Spring 1 to Summer 2 only (20 teaching weeks, Spring 1 has 5). Autumn 1 and 2 are not taught. Maths and English weeks 1 and 2 of Spring 1 are a BRIDGE that covers the essential Year 1 and Year 2 Autumn phonics and number (place value to 100, bonds and addition and subtraction); remaining Autumn content (addition and subtraction with exchange, 2-D and 3-D shape) is caught up inside later blocks (see Maths).
- English: Little Wandle Letters and Sounds. Year 2 opens with a Phase 3 to 5 review, then moves to the Year 2 spelling programme once pupils are secure. Friday writing uses the project's text type.
- Maths: by July pupils read, write, compare and order numbers to 100, add and subtract within 100 with and without exchange, recall the 2, 5 and 10 times tables with division facts, use pounds and pence, measure length, mass, capacity and temperature, find halves, thirds and quarters, tell time to five minutes, read simple pictograms and tally charts, and describe position and turns.
- Qur'an: new hifdh Surahs 104 to 98 and Ayat al-Kursi (آية الكرسي), with Al-Fatihah and 114 to 105 revised weekly; Qaidah (قاعدة) completion: tanween (تنوين), madd letters (مد), shaddah (شدة) and reading from the mushaf (مصحف).
- Islamic Studies: the six pillars of iman (إيمان); stories of the Prophets; the mission in Makkah and the Hijrah (هجرة); purity, prayer and good speech. PSHE follows the money, community, safety and kindness strands of the Tarbiyyah Primary map (no RSE, no puberty).
- End of year: White Rose end-of-year assessment, Little Wandle reading and spelling checks and a hifdh and Qaidah check (no SATs). Pupils who finish ready for Year 3 read fluently with expression, spell the Year 2 common exception words and write several sentences in a sequence with correct punctuation.

## Assessment

- Baseline on entry (induction week, 4 to 8 Jan): Little Wandle grapheme-phoneme and tricky-word check; short reading-aloud check; White Rose style number check (counting to 100, tens and ones, bonds to 10 and 20); Qur'an letters and harakat check; Qaidah placement. Use the results to set groups inside the bridge.
- End of each half term: English (phonics and spelling check, one independent piece of writing); Maths (White Rose end-of-block checks plus a short half-term quiz); Topic (labelled diagram or quiz plus a short talk); Qur'an (recite new surahs without prompting; Qaidah reading check); Islamic Studies and PSHE (short oral quiz).
- End of year (Su2 w4): White Rose end-of-year assessment (spring and summer papers adapted), Little Wandle reading and spelling assessment, a reading fluency check, a hifdh and Qaidah check, Topic knowledge quiz. No SATs are sat.
- Core subject tracking: record English, Maths and Qur'an as working towards, at or above the year's expectations each half term.

# English

## At a glance

- Five 30-minute lessons: Monday to Thursday Little Wandle (Phase 3 to 5 review, then the Year 2 spelling programme once secure, with daily reading practice); Friday writing on the project's text type.
- Year 2 expectations: read words with taught graphemes fluently, read Year 2 common exception words, spell by segmenting and using taught patterns and suffixes, write sentences in sequence with capital letters, full stops, question marks, exclamation marks, commas in lists and apostrophes.
- Home learning: 20 minutes of daily reading aloud to an adult, weekly spelling practice from the week's pattern.
- Poems and rhymes are recited or read, never sung.
- Founding year: Spring 1 weeks 1 and 2 are the BRIDGE (essential Year 1 and Autumn Year 2 phonics); Autumn text types are not taught but recounts, instructions and poetry are practised in later units.

- Text types: recounts, information books, instructions, narrative, poetry, diary entry.
- Spelling: Phase 5 review; adding -ing, -ed, -er, -est.

## Autumn 2: Wriggle & Crawl

- Text types: lists and leaflets, instructions, reviews and information books, poetry, writing for different purposes.
- Spelling: the /j/ sound (ge, dge), the /s/ sound spelled c, kn, gn, wr, and the endings -le, -el, -al, -il.
- Founding year: the spelling patterns are taught in Spring 1 weeks 3 to 5 (marked ESSENTIAL); leaflets reappear in Spring 1; information writing is revisited in Summer 2.

# Maths

## At a glance

- Four 30-minute lessons a week following White Rose Year 2 (public block order: place value, addition and subtraction, shape; money, multiplication and division, length and height, mass, capacity and temperature; fractions, time, statistics, position and direction).
- Founding year: Spring 1 weeks 1 and 2 are the BRIDGE (8 lessons) covering the essential Autumn blocks: place value to 100 and addition and subtraction facts. Addition and subtraction with exchange is caught up in Spring 1 (money) and Spring 2 week 3; shape is caught up in Summer 2 week 3.
- Daily 5-minute home fluency: counting forwards and backwards in 2s, 5s, 10s and 3s (spoken, never sung), bonds to 20, then the 2, 5 and 10 times tables.
- Manipulatives at the kitchen table: pasta or buttons, Lego towers of ten, coins, a paper strip number line, a ruler, kitchen scales and jugs, a paper plate clock; free virtual manipulatives on screen.
- End of year: pupils recall the 2, 5 and 10 times tables, add and subtract two-digit numbers, use pounds and pence, measure, find fractions, tell time to five minutes and read simple charts.

## Autumn 1: Place value; Addition and subtraction

- Key content: numbers to 100 in numerals and words, tens and ones, comparing, ordering, counting in 2s, 5s, 10s and 3s; bonds to 10 and 20 and related facts.
- Pupils compare using <, > and =, and use number lines and partitioning.
- Founding year: covered in the bridge (Spring 1 weeks 1 and 2).

## Autumn 2: Addition and subtraction; Shape

- Key content: adding and subtracting across ten and with exchange, inverse operations; properties of 2-D and 3-D shapes, sorting, patterns.
- Founding year: exchange caught up in Spring 1 week 4 and Spring 2 week 3; shape caught up in Summer 2 week 3.

# Topic

## At a glance

- Two 30-minute lessons a week built on the Cornerstones projects; lesson A and lesson B each serve one named subject (science, history, geography, art or DT). Computing is taught in lesson B every second week (weeks 2, 4 and 6).
- Science: everyday materials and how they change; Will It Degrade?; germs and handwashing; plants and what they need. Working scientifically: ask questions, observe, test fairly, record, say what happened.
- History: castles and castle life; Isambard Kingdom Brunel; change within living memory in the local area.
- Geography: towers and bridges around the world; the local area, maps, keys and aerial images; the continents and oceans; plants of the world.
- Art and DT: colour mixing, food landscapes, still life, urban landscapes, observational drawing; fruit pot, paper bridge, stitching, a fragrant sachet. Home activity each week uses household items; no trips, no visitors.
- Each week has a hands-on kitchen table activity; see the Computing and Art and DT sections for those strands.

## Autumn 1: Bounce

- Key content: floating and sinking, properties of materials, compass points, coastlines, colour wheel, clay balls.
- Founding year: skipped; the colour wheel is taught in Spring 1 week 1, compass points in Summer 1 (maps).

## Autumn 2: Wriggle & Crawl (companion: Where Do Snails Live?)

- Key content: living things and habitats, minibeasts, animals including humans, fieldwork, sketch maps, 3-D models.
- Founding year: skipped; habitats are revisited in Summer 2 extension weeks (plants and where they grow); "Where Do Snails Live?" is not taught.

# Qur'an

# Islamic Studies and PSHE

## At a glance

- One 30-minute lesson a week, alternating: Islamic Studies in odd weeks, PSHE in even weeks of each half term.
- Islamic Studies: the six pillars of iman (إيمان); stories of the Prophets; the mission in Makkah and the Hijrah (هجرة); purity, prayer and good speech.
- PSHE: money, kindness, healthy food, community, staying safe, change; no RSE; no puberty (Year 7).
- No images of prophets, angels or Allah. Stories are told orally with objects (not pictures) and the pupils answer in their own words. Say "peace be upon him" (عليه السلام) after prophets' names and ﷺ after the Prophet Muhammad.
- Do not invent hadith or scholars' words; cite surah:ayah only when certain.
