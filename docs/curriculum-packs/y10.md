# Year 10: overview

## At a glance
- Year 10 starts the two-year Pearson Edexcel International GCSE courses. Every course is taught from the specification pupils sit: English Language B (4EB1), English Literature (4ET1), Mathematics A Higher (4MA1), Science Double Award (4SD0), Islamic Studies (4IS1), and two options from Arabic (4AA1, or GCSE 1AA0 for some learners), Business (4BS1), Computer Science (4CP0) and Geography (4GE1).
- 23 lessons of 50 minutes a week: English 5, Maths 4, Science 5, two options 2 each, Islamic Studies 2, Qur'an 2, PSHE 1. No PE. Qur'an and PSHE are not examined.
- Founding year: induction week Mon 4 to Fri 8 Jan 2027 (accounts, device checks, meet teachers, entry and baseline assessments; no live lessons), first live lessons Mon 18 Jan, 20 live weeks to Fri 9 Jul. The course then has about 52 teaching weeks to the summer 2028 exams, not about 68. Content is front-loaded and each subject has one extra hour of set independent study a week; entries are reviewed in March 2028.
- Founding route (the same for every subject): weeks 1 and 2 teach the essentials of the Autumn units (the maths and English bridge); three of Spring 1 weeks 1 to 4 (Spring 1 has five dated weeks; each subject section names the week that moves to independent study), all of Spring 2, Summer 1 weeks 1 to 5, Summer 2 weeks 1, 2, 5 and 6 are ESSENTIAL and taught live; every other week is EXTENSION: independent study in the founding year, then caught up in the first weeks of Autumn 2027.
- Year 10 mocks: full papers in the week of 28 Jun 2027 (these are the end-of-year tests); reports go to parents in July.
- Exams are sat as private candidates at an exam centre families book (summer 2028 for Year 10; dates TO CONFIRM when Pearson publishes the 2028 timetable). The school gives each family a written entry list per pupil by 30 November of Year 11.
- Arabic entry (4AA1 or GCSE 1AA0) is decided per pupil by July of Year 10.

## Assessment
- Baseline on entry: GL entry assessments, Qur'an recitation placement, Arabic placement and reading age (induction week); then a diagnostic task in each subject in the first week (4EB1 or 4ET1 style extract, 4MA1 style number and algebra paper, science foundations quiz and so on).
- Every lesson: retrieval starter and exit ticket on the big question. End of every unit: short portal quiz (15 to 20 minutes), except PSHE. Every half term: hifdh check and tajweed recitation against the rubric (Qur'an groups).
- End of each half term (founding year): Spring 1 ends Fri 12 Feb; Spring 2 ends Fri 23 Apr; Summer 1 ends Fri 11 Jun; Summer 2 ends Fri 9 Jul.
- Assessment point 2: late April (week of 26 Apr), core subject tests (English, Maths, Science) and a report to parents. Assessment point 3: June, end-of-year tests in every subject, which for Year 10 are the mocks below; report in July.
- Year 10 mock papers (Mon 28 to Wed 30 Jun 2027 proposed, TO CONFIRM): English Language 4EB1 style 3-hour paper (Section B as a baseline); English Literature Component 1 (2 hours: unseen, anthology, Of Mice and Men); Maths 4MA1 style Papers 1H and 2H (2 hours each, split over the week); Science 4SD0 style 1B, 1C and 1P (2 hours each, split over the week); Islamic Studies Section A and 3.1 to 3.5 (2 hours); options: Arabic Group A Paper 1 style (2 hours 15) and a Paper 2 task, Group B 1AA0 Papers 1, 3 and 4 style, Business Papers 1 and 2 style, Computer Science Paper 1 style (2 hours) and a Python task, Geography Paper 1 style (1 hour 10) and a Section C question on fragile environments and climate change.
- Teachers record one judgement per subject at each point: working towards, at or above the expected standard for the year. Pupils working towards get a support plan before the next point.
- All tests run in the portal's secure test mode where the format allows.

# English Language (4EB1)

## At a glance
- Taught from Pearson Edexcel International GCSE English Language B (4EB1): one 3-hour paper, 100 marks, no set texts, no coursework. Section A reading on two unseen extracts (40 marks), Section B directed writing (30), Section C one discursive, narrative or descriptive piece from a choice of three (30).
- Year 10 builds reading of non-fiction (meaning, language, structure, comparing writers' views) and narrative and descriptive writing. Year 11 adds directed writing, discursive writing and full 3-hour papers.
- Suggested share of the 5 English lessons: Language 3, Literature 2 (TO CONFIRM: split).
- Portal lessons are framed on AQA 8700 question numbers; teach the skills, drop the question numbers and use 4EB1 past papers and mark schemes.
- The school's 2026-27 KS4 overviews and Language schemes (Year 10 HT1 'Understanding and analysing texts', HT6 Paper 2 and spoken language) are also framed on AQA question numbers: the reading and writing skills transfer to 4EB1 Sections A to C; the question numbers do not.

## Autumn 1: Reading non-fiction: meaning and evidence
- Explicit and implicit information, selecting and quoting evidence, summarising; unseen non-fiction from the 19th, 20th and 21st centuries.
- Active reading and annotation; vocabulary in context.
- Baseline: one Section A style extract and one short Section C task.
- Founding year: Reading core: retrieval, inference and evidence on a 4EB1 style extract (bridge week, 11 Jan).

## Autumn 2: Reading non-fiction: language and structure
- How word choice, tone, imagery and rhetoric shape a reader; how openings, shifts and endings are structured.
- Subject terminology that supports analysis, with effect leading the sentence.
- Founding year: Language and structure core: analysing an extract in a paragraph (bridge week, 18 Jan).

# English Literature (4ET1)

## At a glance
- Taught from Pearson Edexcel International GCSE English Literature (4ET1). Component 1 (4ET1/01): 2 hours, 90 marks, 60%, closed book: unseen poetry (20), anthology poetry (30), modern prose (40). Component 2 (4ET1/02): 1 hour 30, 60 marks, 40%, open book: modern drama (30) and a literary heritage text (30).
- Order and set texts follow the school's Tarbiyyah Secondary English Language and Literature scheme of work 2026-27 (Year 10 HT1 to HT6 overviews) where they are on 4ET1: An Inspector Calls (modern drama) and Macbeth (literary heritage) in the school's order; Of Mice and Men (modern prose) from the school's KS4 English Literature scheme. Pearson's generic 4ET1 scheme of work (in the school folder) supports the prose and poetry units.
- Not adopted: A Christmas Carol (school Year 10 HT1) is not a 4ET1 set text and is about a Christmas celebration; the school's 'Edexcel Conflict Anthology' (HT4 and HT5) is the UK GCSE cluster, not the 4ET1 anthology. Its comparison method (TAPS, PEMAACS) is kept and the 16 4ET1 anthology poems are taught instead. War Photographer on 4ET1 is Carol Ann Duffy's poem, not Carole Satyamurti's.
- TO CONFIRM: text suitability of Of Mice and Men (racist slurs, references to a brothel, violence and a mercy killing) and An Inspector Calls (Eva Smith's pregnancy, Eric's drinking and his treatment of Eva); poems with romantic imagery (Sonnet 116, La Belle Dame sans Merci) taught as literary analysis. Component 2 is the working route.

## Autumn 1: Modern prose: Of Mice and Men (Component 1 Section C)
- 1930s America, the Great Depression and the American Dream (AO4); plot, characters, themes, structure and Steinbeck's methods.
- The 40-mark closed-book essay (AO1, AO4): short memorable quotations, argument and context.
- Resources: the school's KS4 'Of Mice and Men' scheme of work; Pearson 4ET1 scheme of work (modern prose section); portal english-literature y10 lessons 30 to 35.
- Founding year: Of Mice and Men core: context, plot, characters and themes (bridge week of 11 Jan); the novel is read at home; the full unit is caught up in the first weeks of Autumn 2027.

## Autumn 2: Modern drama: An Inspector Calls (Component 2 Section A)
- Act 1 to Act 3 in the school's order: the Birlings, Mr Birling and Eva Smith, Sheila, Gerald, Mrs Birling, Eric and the Inspector's final speech; responsibility, class and power.
- The open-book 30-mark answer (AO1, AO2); reading the play as performance.
- Resources: Tarbiyyah Secondary English Language and Literature scheme of work 2026-27, Year 10 HT2 (7 weeks); a clean prescribed edition for the open-book paper.
- Founding year: An Inspector Calls core (bridge week of 18 Jan); the play is read at home; the full unit is caught up in Autumn 2027.

## At a glance
- Not examined. One lesson a week. PSHE only: no relationships and sex education; the fiqh of puberty is taught separately to boys and girls from Year 7.
- Year 10: managing stress and exam pressure; staying safe; the world of work and careers. No work experience visits: careers research is done online and at home.
- Portal lessons: pshe y10 lessons 1 to 4. TO CONFIRM: reword the portal lesson "Healthy relationships, marriage and the law" (y10 lesson 2) to fit the no-RSE rule.

## Autumn 1: Health and wellbeing
- Managing stress and exam pressure; sleep, routine, screen time.
- Founding year: Stress and exam pressure core (week of 11 Jan).

## Autumn 2: Relationships and respect (PSHE only)
- Respect in family, friendship and online; the law and responsibility.
- Founding year: Respect and responsibility core (week of 18 Jan).
