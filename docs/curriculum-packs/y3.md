# Year 3: overview

## At a glance
- Tarbiyyah Global Academy (TGA) Year 3: 20 live lessons a week of 45 minutes on Microsoft Teams, maximum 15 pupils. English 5, Maths 5, Science 2, History and geography 2, Computing or Art and DT 1 (Computing in Autumn 1, Spring 1 and Summer 1; Art and DT in Autumn 2, Spring 2 and Summer 2), Qur'an and Tajweed 2, Arabic 1, Islamic Studies 1, PSHE 1.
- Project order follows Tarbiyyah Primary's Long Term Plan 2026-2027 exactly: Predator!, Scrumdiddlyumptious!, Mighty Metals, Tribal Tales, Flow, Tremors, each with its companion projects.
- Schemes: Cornerstones Maestro projects and English units; White Rose Maths Year 3; Kitabi Arabic (placed by level); school Islamic Studies and PSHE materials.
- Founding year: induction week Mon 4 to Fri 8 Jan 2027 (accounts, device checks, meet teachers, baseline assessments; no live lessons); first live lessons Mon 11 Jan 2027, teaching Spring 1 to Summer 2 only (20 weeks, 94 teaching days; Spring 1 has 5). Weeks 11 Jan and 18 Jan are an English and maths "bridge" of missed Autumn essentials. Each half term is planned as 6 weeks; ESSENTIAL and EXTENSION weeks are marked so Summer 1 (5 dated weeks) and Summer 2 (4 dated weeks) fit.
- Qur'an: new hifdh Al-Qadr, Al-Alaq, At-Tin, Ash-Sharh, Ad-Duha (97 to 93); tajweed noon sakinah, tanween and meem sakinah.
- End of year (no SATs; TGA is not a state school): pupils read fluently and write organised paragraphs; know place value to 1,000, written methods, 2 to 10 tables (2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 10 secure), fractions, measures and time; have secured the year's science, history and geography; recite 97 to 93 with the year's tajweed. Measured by White Rose and reading assessments.
- Home learning: daily reading 20 minutes, Qur'an revision 10 to 15 minutes, weekly maths and spelling practice.
- House rules: no music or songs, no trips or visitors, no PE, no RSE or puberty content, no images of prophets or of Allah.

# English

## At a glance
- Five 45-minute live lessons a week: 2 reading (shared text, vocabulary, comprehension), 1 grammar and spelling, 2 writing (plan and draft; edit and publish). Each half term follows the project's text types from the Cornerstones English units.
- Year 3 writing builds from sentences to organised paragraphs: conjunctions and adverbs for time, place and cause; prepositions; headings and sub-headings; direct speech punctuation; the present perfect; choosing a or an. Spelling follows the Year 3/4 statutory list plus prefixes (un-, dis-, mis-, re-, in-, im-, il-, ir-) and suffixes (-ly, -ous, -tion, -sion, -ssion, -cian).
- Handwriting: joined, legible writing practised in a 10-minute daily slot at home; pupils hold up work to the camera for checks.
- End of year: pupils write non-fiction (report, explanation, instructions, letter, newspaper report) and narrative (adventure, historical) in well-organised paragraphs, proofread their own work, read fluently, and discuss books, poems and non-fiction using evidence from the text.
- Home learning: 20 minutes of reading a day, weekly spelling practice (10 words), one short piece of writing practice a week.
- No songs: poems are performed as spoken verse with rhythm and intonation.

## Autumn 1: Predator!
- Dictionaries and thesauri, class glossary; non-chronological reports with headings, labels, diagrams; persuasive leaflets on a chosen predator; haiku (inspired by Tennyson's poem The Eagle); a dilemma story and a speech.
- Founding year: not taught. Essentials (report organisation, dictionary use, sentence variety) are covered in bridge week 1.

## Autumn 2: Scrumdiddlyumptious!
- Recounts of a (virtual) food investigation; reading and writing recipes and instructions; nonsense poetry with invented food words; a biography-based report on a pioneer of nutrition; persuasive adverts.
- Founding year: not taught. Recounts, instructions and persuasion are covered in bridge week 2 and revisited in Mighty Metals (instructions) and Tremors (recount).

# Maths

## At a glance
- Follows White Rose Maths Year 3 in Tarbiyyah Primary's block order: five 45-minute live lessons a week, each built on one or two White Rose small steps, with a 5-minute fluency starter (times tables, number bonds).
- By July pupils read, write, compare and order numbers to 1,000; add and subtract up to three digits with columns; know the 2, 3, 4, 5, 8 and 10 times tables; multiply and divide two-digit by one-digit numbers; use unit, non-unit and equivalent fractions and tenths; measure length, perimeter, mass, capacity, money and time; read and draw bar charts and pictograms.
- Practical kit at the kitchen table: ruler, string, kitchen scales, measuring jug, coins, paper clock, fraction strips cut from paper.
- Home learning: 10 minutes of times tables a day (spoken chants only), one weekly practice sheet.

## Autumn 1: Place value; Addition and subtraction (start)
- Numbers to 1,000: represent, partition, number lines, compare, order, count in 50s; add and subtract 1s, 10s, 100s.
- Founding year: not taught. Bridge week 1 (11 Jan) covers place value to 1,000.

## Autumn 2: Addition and subtraction; Multiplication and division A
- Column addition and subtraction with exchange, estimating and checking; equal groups, arrays, the 2, 5, 10, 3, 4 and 8 times tables.
- Founding year: not taught. Bridge week 2 (18 Jan) covers written methods and the 3, 4 and 8 tables.

# Islamic Studies and PSHE

## At a glance
- Islamic Studies: one 45-minute lesson a week. Order: tawheed (Autumn 1 and 2); the Prophet ﷺ in Madinah (Spring 1); wudu and the adhan (Spring 2); intention and gratitude (Summer 1); the Madinah community and review (Summer 2).
- PSHE: one 45-minute lesson a week, using the school's PSHE units: We are all stars; Be friendly, be wise; Living long, living strong; Being different; Dear diary; Joining in and joining up. There is no RSE and no puberty content.
- Pupils never draw or act the Prophets ﷺ or the Companions رضي الله عنهم; stories are told orally and with maps and photographs of places.
- Qur'an and hadith are quoted only from the school's approved texts. TO CONFIRM: which seerah and hadith texts the school uses.
- Founding year: Autumn units are not taught; Spring 1 week 1 gives a tawheed bridge.
