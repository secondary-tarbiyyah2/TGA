# Year 5: overview

## At a glance
- 20 live lessons a week of 45 minutes, max 15 per class on Microsoft Teams: English 5, Maths 5, Science 2, History and geography 2, Computing or Art and DT 1 (Computing in Autumn 1, Spring 1 and Summer 1; Art and DT in Autumn 2, Spring 2 and Summer 2), Qur'an and Tajweed 2, Arabic 1, Islamic Studies 1, PSHE 1. No PE.
- Projects follow Tarbiyyah Primary's Long Term Plan 2026-2027 exactly: Scream Machine, Stargazers, Alchemy Island, Ground-Breaking Greeks, Allotment, Time Traveller, each with its companion projects (see the table below).
- Schemes: White Rose Maths Year 5, Cornerstones Maestro projects (lesson detail: CHECK IN MAESTRO), Kitabi Arabic. Home learning: daily reading 20 minutes, Qur'an revision 10-15 minutes, weekly maths and spelling practice.
- End-of-year expectations: fluent written methods and fraction/decimal/percentage links in maths; independent writing across the Year 5 text types; secure working-scientifically skills; Year 5 hifdh (Surahs 88 to 85) with tajweed (makharij, laam rules, stopping signs); simple spoken and written Arabic about oneself, home and likes.
- No SATs: TGA is not a state school. Progress is checked with White Rose end-of-block and end-of-year papers, a standardised reading test (TO CONFIRM provider) and teacher assessment.
- Founding year: induction week Mon 4 to Fri 8 Jan 2027 (accounts, device checks, meet teachers, baseline assessments; no live lessons); first live lessons Mon 11 Jan 2027; pupils learn Spring 1 to Summer 2 only (20 teaching weeks; Spring 1 has 5). Maths and English use bridge weeks on 11 Jan and 18 Jan for missed Autumn essentials. Shortened weeks and the Spring 2 condensed week are marked below.

# English

## At a glance
- Five 45-minute live lessons a week: 2 reading (whole-class text, then comprehension and discussion), 2 writing (plan, draft, edit on the unit's text type), 1 grammar and spelling. Daily home reading of 20 minutes.
- Writing follows the Tarbiyyah Primary text types for each project, with the Year 5 grammar spine: relative clauses, modal verbs, parenthesis (brackets, commas, dashes), adverbials of time, place and number, cohesion across paragraphs, and standard punctuation for direct speech.
- Spelling follows the Year 5/6 national curriculum patterns: -ous, -tion/-sion/-ssion/-cian, -cious/-tious, -ant/-ance/-ent/-ence, -able/-ible, silent letters, homophones, prefixes (dis-, mis-, in-, il-, im-, ir-, re-) and suffix rules. No phonics scheme is needed in Year 5.
- By July pupils write for different purposes with a clear structure, edit their own work, read fluently with expression and discuss what they read with evidence. Pupils perform spoken work (soliloquy, play script, poetry) without music or song.
- Class reader for each half term: TO CONFIRM (choose a whole text that fits the project and the house rules).

## Autumn 1: Scream Machine and Pharaohs
- Scream Machine (text types from the school's Year 5 map; no school English scheme for this project was among the files): poetry about movement and speed; a short narrative with dialogue (a ride that goes wrong); signage and formal emails (park rules); an advert for a new ride; non-fiction book features (contents, index, headings).
- Pharaohs (school English scheme order): a chronological report of Howard Carter's discovery of Tutankhamun's tomb (1922); a fact file on one group in Egyptian society; a group presentation on everyday life; a mystery story that begins with the discovery of a tomb; a play script with stage directions and adverbs. Not adopted: acting out the story of the god Osiris (the play script is written about Carter's discovery instead).
- Founding year: skipped. Essentials (direct speech punctuation, paragraphing, formal and informal register) are covered in the Spring 1 bridge weeks.

## Autumn 2: Stargazers
- Mnemonics for the planets and the phases of the Moon; free verse poetry about the night sky; a newspaper report (the Moon landing or Galileo's discoveries); science fiction or graphic narrative.
- Grammar focus: relative clauses, modal verbs for possibility (might, could, will probably).
- Founding year: skipped. Newspaper-report features are revisited in Summer 2 (fact files).

# Maths

## At a glance
- Five 45-minute live lessons a week following White Rose Maths Year 5 in its published block order (Autumn: place value, addition and subtraction, multiplication and division A, fractions A; Spring: multiplication and division B, fractions B, decimals and percentages, perimeter and area, statistics; Summer: shape, position and direction, decimals, negative numbers, converting units, volume).
- Weekly pattern: fluency starter, one new small step per lesson, reasoning and problem solving on the last lesson of the week; weekly times-tables and arithmetic practice at home (multiplication facts to 12 x 12 secure by July).
- Concrete and pictorial resources are household: counters from coins or beans, paper strips for fractions, squared paper, a ruler.
- By July pupils work with numbers to 1,000,000, use formal written methods, add and subtract fractions, link fractions, decimals and percentages, find area and perimeter, measure and draw angles, plot coordinates and convert units.

## Autumn 1: Place value, addition and subtraction
- Numbers to 1,000,000: read, write, compare, order, round, Roman numerals; counting in powers of 10.
- Written addition and subtraction with more than four digits; estimate and check with the inverse; multi-step problems.
- Founding year: skipped; place value and written methods are revisited in bridge week 1 (11 Jan).

## Autumn 2: Multiplication and division A, fractions A
- Multiples, factors, common factors, prime numbers, square and cube numbers; multiply and divide by 10, 100 and 1,000.
- Fractions A: equivalent fractions, improper fractions and mixed numbers, comparing and ordering, adding and subtracting fractions with related denominators.
- Founding year: skipped; factors, multiples and equivalent fractions are revisited in bridge week 2 (18 Jan).

# Islamic Studies and PSHE

## At a glance
- Two 45-minute live lessons a week: one Islamic Studies and one PSHE, taught as a pair (each week's row gives both).
- Islamic Studies Year 5: tawheed and qadar; the Prophet's ﷺ journey from Ta'if to the Hijrah; zakah, sawm and Hajj; truthful speech, online too. Hadith and ayat are cited only when the teacher has checked the source: TO CONFIRM who vets the source list.
- PSHE follows the Tarbiyyah Primary themes (teamwork, friendship and conflict, being an effective learner, difference of opinion, getting help, rules and laws). Puberty is not taught in primary.
- The calendar is fitted to the year: sawm is taught before Ramadan, zakah after, Hajj before Eid al-Adha.

## Autumn 1: Tawheed; We're All Stars
- Islamic Studies: what La ilaha illa Allah means; tawheed ar-rububiyyah, al-uluhiyyah and al-asma wa as-sifat in simple terms; the beautiful names Ar-Rahman (الرحمن), Al-Khaliq (الخالق), As-Sami' (السميع), Al-Basir (البصير); shirk and living by tawakkul.
- PSHE: working cooperatively; exploring and naming feelings.
- Founding year: skipped. A short tawheed recap opens Spring 1 week 1.

## Autumn 2: Qadar and truthfulness; Be Friendly, Be Wise
- Islamic Studies: belief in al-qadar (القدر): knowledge, writing, will and creation; effort and trust together; patience and gratitude; honest speech begins.
- PSHE: friendship and conflict resolution; managing anger; anti-bullying, including online.
- Founding year: skipped. Qadar is revisited in Summer 1 week 4 with the Hajj theme (trust and patience).
