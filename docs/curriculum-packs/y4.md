# Year 4: overview

## At a glance

- 20 live lessons a week of 45 minutes on Microsoft Teams (max 15 per class): English 5, Maths 5, Science 2, History and geography 2, Computing OR Art and DT 1 (alternating by half term), Qur'an and Tajweed 2, Arabic 1, Islamic Studies 1, PSHE 1.
- Follows Tarbiyyah Primary's Long Term Plan 2026-2027 project order exactly: Traders & Raiders, Digest This!, Marvellous Medicine, Misty Mountain, Sierra, Blue Abyss, Road Trip USA, with the companion projects carrying science, art and DT.
- Maths follows White Rose Year 4 block order. Times tables: weekly practice (two 5-minute starters, one home check, a Friday 10-question quiz) building to full 12 x 12 recall, with a full recall check in the week of 7 June.
- English uses the Cornerstones project texts as the reading spine, with Year 4 grammar and spelling taught weekly (fronted adverbials, speech punctuation, paragraphs, Standard English, prefixes and suffixes, homophones).
- Qur'an: new memorisation Surahs 92 to 89 (Al-Layl, Ash-Shams, Al-Balad, Al-Fajr) and Al-Baqarah 285-286, revising all earlier surahs. Tajweed: heavy and light letters, idgham, families of madd.
- Founding year: induction week Mon 4 to Fri 8 Jan 2027 (accounts, device checks, meet teachers, baseline assessments; no live lessons); first live lessons Mon 11 Jan 2027. Spring 1 to Summer 2 only, 20 teaching weeks (Spring 1 has 5). Spring 1 weeks 1 and 2 (11 and 18 Jan) are a maths and English "bridge" of missed Autumn essentials.
- End-of-year expectation: secure written methods for four operations with 4-digit numbers, recall of all tables to 12 x 12, fractions and decimals (tenths and hundredths), fluent reading with inference, and writing across the Year 4 text types. TGA is not a state school: no statutory Year 4 times tables check; the school uses an internal check in the same format (TO CONFIRM).
- Home learning: daily reading 20 minutes, Qur'an revision 10 to 15 minutes, weekly maths (times tables) and spelling practice.

## Assessment

- Baseline on entry (induction week, 4 to 8 Jan): English (reading comprehension paper plus a short independent writing sample); Maths (White Rose-style Year 3 end-of-year check, plus a times table check on 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 10); Qur'an (Qaidah and tajweed check, surahs known); Arabic (letters and word recognition, for placement); no baseline for foundation subjects beyond a short teacher discussion.
- End of each half term: English (assessed piece of writing in the half term's text type, marked against a Year 4 rubric, plus reading questions); Maths (White Rose end-of-block assessments for the blocks covered, plus the weekly Friday times table quiz tracked on one sheet); Science and History/Geography (short knowledge quiz and one working-scientifically or enquiry task); Qur'an (hifdh recital and tajweed check).
- English: spelling and grammar tests each half term on the taught patterns; reading tracked through a termly comprehension paper (TO CONFIRM which published paper) and teacher records of fluency.
- Maths: June times table check in the week of 7 June (25 questions, about 6 seconds each, same format as the national check, internal only); repeat in the week of 14 June for pupils who need it.
- End of year (week of 5 July): White Rose end-of-year assessment, a reading comprehension paper and a writing assessment, a hifdh and tajweed recitation, an Arabic check, and a short science and humanities quiz. Year 4 has no statutory test; results go in the end-of-year report.

# English

## At a glance

- 5 lessons a week of 45 minutes: 2 reading (fluency, retrieval, inference, vocabulary), 1 grammar and spelling, 2 writing (plan, draft, edit). Daily home reading 20 minutes and a weekly spelling list.
- Each half term follows its Cornerstones project texts and text types; the class reader and key texts are listed in each unit (CHECK IN MAESTRO for the project texts).
- Year 4 grammar: noun phrases, fronted adverbials and commas, direct speech punctuation, paragraphs, pronouns for cohesion, Standard English verb forms, possessive apostrophes. Spelling: prefixes (in-, il-, im-, ir-, sub-, inter-, super-, anti-, auto-), suffixes (-ation, -ous, -ly, -sure, -ture, -sion, -tion, -cian), homophones, statutory word list.
- Handwriting: joined, legible, consistent; presented work shared by camera or typed.

## Autumn 1: Traders & Raiders

- Historical narrative set in an Anglo-Saxon or Viking village; character profiles; myths and legends (a retelling of a Beowulf-style tale, TO CONFIRM text); praise poems as skalds (spoken, never sung) using rhyme, metaphor and personification; information reports on Vikings and Saxons.
- Grammar focus: expanded noun phrases, fronted adverbials, paragraphs, speech punctuation.
- Founding year: covered in the bridge (Spring 1 weeks 1 and 2: sentence toolkit and a historical story); skald poems revisited in Summer 1 poetry.

## Autumn 2: Digest This!

- Fact files on teeth and digestion, explanations using idioms, a fantasy narrative (a journey through the digestive system), slogans and persuasive texts on healthy eating.
- Grammar focus: Standard English, pronouns, conjunctions of cause and time, persuasive devices.
- Founding year: covered in the bridge (fact file in week 2); persuasion revisited in Summer 1 persuasive letters.

# Maths

## At a glance

- 5 lessons a week of 45 minutes on White Rose Year 4 in Tarbiyyah Primary's block order; each lesson is one small step, with a 5-minute fluency starter.
- Times-table thread: two 5-minute starters, a home practice sheet and a Friday 10-question quiz every week, building to full 12 x 12 recall (multiplication and division facts). The June check (week of 7 June) is a 25-question, 6-seconds-each check modelled on the national format, internal only. Pupils record scores on one tracker sheet.
- Online manipulatives: place value counters, base 10, number lines and fraction walls (free virtual versions on screen); at the table, pupils use coins, paper strips, squared paper, a ruler and an egg box.
- By July: 4-digit written methods, tables to 12 x 12, fractions and decimals (tenths and hundredths), perimeter and area, money, time to the minute and 24-hour clock, statistics, shape, angles and coordinates.

- Numbers to 10,000, partitioning, rounding to 10, 100, 1,000, Roman numerals, then written addition and subtraction with exchange.

# Islamic Studies and PSHE

## At a glance

- Islamic Studies 1 lesson a week and PSHE 1 lesson a week, each 45 minutes. Year 4 Islamic Studies: Islam, Iman and Ihsan; the Makkan years in depth; the pillars of salah and praying in congregation; trust, family and neighbours.
- All Arabic terms are shown in Arabic script beside the transliteration; no images or drawings of the prophets or of Allah; stories are told in words only.
- Qur'an references are given only where certain (surah:ayah); no hadith are quoted unless checked against a reliable source (TO CONFIRM source texts with the Islamic Studies lead).
- PSHE is called "PSHE" only; there is no RSE and no puberty teaching at TGA primary. Units cover rules, safety, money, consumers, respect, change and online safety.

## Autumn 1: Islam, Iman and Ihsan; Rules, law and caring for the earth

- Islam (الإسلام), Iman (الإيمان), Ihsan (الإحسان) introduced through the well-known hadith of Jibril (text TO CONFIRM). PSHE: why we have rules and laws; looking after the earth as a trust.
- Founding year: covered in the bridge in Spring 1 weeks 1 and 2.

## Autumn 2: Iman and Ihsan in action; Keeping safe

- The six articles of iman named; ihsan as doing our best as if Allah sees us. PSHE: keeping safe with medicines and harmful substances (factual, age-appropriate), online safety, cyberbullying and spotting fake content.
- Founding year: skipped; online safety revisited in Summer 2.
