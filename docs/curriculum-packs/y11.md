# Year 11: overview

## At a glance
- Year 11 completes the two-year Pearson Edexcel International GCSE courses: English Language B (4EB1), English Literature (4ET1), Mathematics A Higher (4MA1), Science Double Award (4SD0), Islamic Studies (4IS1), and two options from Arabic (4AA1, or GCSE 1AA0), Business (4BS1), Computer Science (4CP0) and Geography (4GE1). Qur'an and PSHE continue and are not examined.
- 23 lessons of 50 minutes a week: English 5, Maths 4, Science 5, two options 2 each, Islamic Studies 2, Qur'an 2, PSHE 1. No PE.
- Normal year: Autumn teaches the remaining new units, Spring is two revision cycles on the full specification with full papers, mocks in December and before the Ramadan break, Summer 1 is final papers, then the exam period. Summer 2 is after the exams: no live Year 11 lessons (TO CONFIRM: Year 11 leaving date).
- Founding year (2027): only pupils already on the matching International GCSE courses are admitted, because 19 weeks is not enough to learn a course. They get a revision and exam technique programme on the whole specification: 14 live weeks from Mon 4 Jan to Fri 7 May, then exam leave and optional clinics. A pupil not on the matching course is offered Year 10, or a later series (November 2027 where the subject runs then, otherwise summer 2028).
- June 2027 dates come from Pearson's final summer 2027 timetable (opened Nov 2026): exams run Mon 10 May to Fri 18 Jun for all Pearson International GCSEs. The school's courses fall between Mon 10 May and Wed 9 Jun (see the exam table below). Pearson's key dates give entry deadlines of 21 Feb 2027 for UK centres and 21 Mar 2027 for international centres, and results on 18 Aug (centres) and 19 Aug (candidates). Private candidate deadlines at each centre: TO CONFIRM.
- The school gives each family a written entry list per pupil by 30 November of Year 11 in a normal year. In 2027 the UK centre entry deadline (21 Feb) falls inside the Ramadan break (15 Feb to 12 Mar), so entry lists should reach families in the first week back (TO CONFIRM).
- Exams are sat as private candidates at a centre that families book; candidates must sit each paper on the day and in the session Pearson sets. The Eid al-Adha half term (10 to 21 May) overlaps the exam period; TO CONFIRM how Eid days and the centres' dates are handled.
- Year 11 mocks: full papers in December and again before the Ramadan break; in the founding year only the second, in the week of 8 Feb.

- A pupil who is not on track after the mocks gets a support plan; entry reviews with parents follow the mock results.

# English Language (4EB1)

## At a glance
- Exam: Pearson Edexcel International GCSE English Language B (4EB1), one 3-hour paper, 100 marks: Section A reading on two unseen extracts (40), Section B directed writing (30), Section C one piece from a choice of three (30). June 2027 paper: Mon 24 May, morning (Pearson final timetable). Optional Spoken Language Endorsement.
- Year 11 adds directed writing (audience, form, purpose, using ideas from the texts) and discursive writing, then full 3-hour papers from Spring.
- Suggested share of the 5 English lessons: Language 3, Literature 2 (TO CONFIRM).
- Founding year: revision and exam technique on the whole paper for pupils already on the course (see the week by week table).

## Autumn 1: Directed writing (Section B)
- Writing for a given audience, form and purpose using ideas from two source texts (letter, article, speech, report; forms TO CONFIRM from past papers).
- Selecting, adapting and organising ideas; register and tone.
- 2027-28 cohort only: first two weeks revisit the Year 10 EXTENSION weeks of reading and writing.
- Founding year: not taught as new content; revised in Spring 1 (S1 w4).

## Autumn 2: Discursive writing (Section C) and speaking
- Discursive writing: balanced argument, viewpoint, rhetorical craft; choosing among discursive, narrative and descriptive tasks.
- Spoken Language Endorsement (optional presentation of up to 10 minutes): planning and delivery. TO CONFIRM: whether the exam centre runs it.
- December mock: full 3-hour paper.
- Founding year: not taught as new content; revised in Spring 1 (S1 w5).

# Mathematics (4MA1 Higher)

## At a glance
- Exam: Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A (4MA1) Higher: Paper 1H Fri 14 May and Paper 2H Thu 27 May 2027, both mornings, 2 hours, 100 marks, calculator allowed, formulae sheet supplied.
- Year 11: trigonometry; vectors and transformations; statistics and probability; calculus (4MA1 only: differentiation of integer powers, turning points, simple kinematics, 3.4); calculator papers. Set language and Venn diagrams (1.5) are examined formally.
- Practise full 2-hour calculator papers from the start of Year 11; keep non-calculator fluency. 1MA1-only content (box plots, iteration, exponential graphs) is not examined.

## Autumn 1: Trigonometry, measures and vectors
- Pythagoras and trigonometry (G20 to G23): right-angled, sine and cosine rules, area of a triangle, 3D problems; circles, solids and units revisited (G9 to G14); measures and accuracy (N13 to N16).
- Vectors (G24, G25): notation, vector geometry and proof.
- 2027-28 cohort only: first two weeks revisit Year 10 EXTENSION weeks (surface area, volume, similarity, inequalities on graphs).
- Founding year: not taught as new content; revised in S1 and S2.

## Autumn 2: Transformations, statistics, probability and calculus
- Transformations (translation, reflection, rotation, enlargement); harder graphs and transformations of graphs (A13 to A16); proportional equations (R13 to R16).
- Statistics (S1 to S6): averages, cumulative frequency, histograms, scatter graphs; probability (P1 to P9): trees, conditional, sets and Venn.
- Calculus (4MA1 3.4): differentiation of integer powers, gradients, turning points, kinematics.
- Founding year: not taught as new content; revised in S2.
