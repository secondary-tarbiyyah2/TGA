# Year 6: overview

## At a glance

- 20 live lessons a week of 45 minutes on Microsoft Teams (max 15 per class): English 5, Maths 5, Science 2, History and geography 2, Computing OR Art and DT 1, Qur'an and Tajweed 2, Arabic 1, Islamic Studies 1, PSHE 1. Computing runs in Autumn 1, Spring 1 and Summer 1; Art and DT runs in Autumn 2, Spring 2 and Summer 2.
- Follows Tarbiyyah Primary's Long Term Plan 2026-2027 project order exactly: Blood Heart, A Child's War, Frozen Kingdom, Tomorrow's World, Revolution, ID. The main project carries history and geography; companion projects carry science, art and DT.
- Maths follows White Rose Year 6 (place value, four operations, fractions, converting units, ratio, algebra, decimals, percentages, area, perimeter and volume, statistics, shape, position and direction). English follows each project's text types. Arabic follows Kitabi, with pupils placed by level.
- Qur'an: new hifdh this year is Surahs 84 to 81 (Al-Inshiqaq, Al-Mutaffifin, Al-Infitar, At-Takwir), with all earlier surahs revised. Tajweed focus: the sifat of the letters and reciting with beauty.
- Islamic Studies: the names and attributes of Allah; the pillars of iman in depth; the four Rightly Guided Caliphs; fiqh and akhlaq. The Prophet's life is not retaught (Years 1, 4 and 5 cover it). Summer 2 includes transition to secondary (Year 7 at TGA).
- Year 6 SATs are not sat (TGA is not a state school). End of year: White Rose end-of-year maths assessment, a reading comprehension assessment, a grammar, punctuation and spelling check and a writing portfolio, to show readiness for Year 7.
- Founding year: first live lessons Mon 4 Jan 2027; teaches Spring 1 to Summer 2 only (21 dated weeks). Autumn essentials are bridged in maths and English in the first two weeks.
- Home learning: daily reading 20 minutes, Qur'an revision 10 to 15 minutes, weekly maths and spelling practice.

## Assessment

- Baseline on entry (first week): White Rose Year 5 end-of-year style arithmetic and reasoning check; reading comprehension; short unaided writing; spelling check; Qur'an placement listening (surahs known, tajweed); Arabic Kitabi placement.

- Maths: White Rose end-of-block checks; end-of-year White Rose assessment (Summer 2 week 5). Science: working scientifically task each half term.
- End of year: reports using the above; Year 7 transition summary for each pupil.

# English

## At a glance

- Five 45-minute lessons a week: reading comprehension and discussion (2), writing on the project's text type (2), grammar, punctuation and spelling (1). Each project supplies the text types; Tarbiyyah Primary's English schemes of work for the six projects guide the sequence. TO CONFIRM: the class texts for 2026-27 (CHECK IN MAESTRO).
- By July pupils write for a clear purpose and audience, control tense and formality, use the passive, relative clauses, modal verbs, cohesive devices, hyphens, colons, semi-colons and bullet points correctly, and edit their own work.
- Reading: pupils retrieve, infer, summarise, comment on word choice and compare texts, and read 20 minutes a day at home. Spelling follows the Year 5 and 6 statutory word lists, with a weekly practice list.
- Spoken English: discussion, debate, speeches, and spoken podcast scripts and chants (no music).

# Maths

## At a glance

- Five 45-minute lessons a week following White Rose Year 6: roughly a fluency starter, a worked example, guided practice and independent practice.
- By July pupils work with numbers to 10,000,000, use long multiplication and division, calculate with fractions, decimals and percentages, use ratio and simple algebra, convert units, find area, perimeter and volume, interpret pie charts and means, find missing angles and plot in four quadrants.
- Weekly home practice: one fluency sheet (times tables and arithmetic), plus a reasoning problem.
- Manipulatives at the kitchen table: paper, ruler, squared paper, dried pasta, measuring jugs, scales and a home-made protractor from a printed template.
- End-of-year White Rose assessment, not SATs.

**Autumn blocks (not taught live in the founding year).** Order: Place value; Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division; Fractions A; Fractions B; Converting units.

# Science

## At a glance

- Two 45-minute lessons a week, built on the companion projects and the national curriculum for Year 6: animals including humans (circulation, diet, exercise, drugs), living things and their habitats (classification), evolution and inheritance, light, electricity, and working scientifically.
- Practical work uses a kitchen table and household items; no dissection. Heart and pulse work uses a stopwatch or phone timer and a pulse count.
- Pupils plan fair tests, choose equipment, record results in tables and graphs, and draw evidence-based conclusions.
- Teacher demonstrates anything needing heat or mains electricity on screen; pupils use a torch, batteries, bulb or LED and wires only at low voltage with an adult.

## Autumn 1: Blood Heart (human circulatory system)

- Parts and jobs of the heart, blood vessels and blood; pulse and heart rate; effect of diet, exercise and drugs; blood groups history.
- Founding year: skipped; heart rate and healthy lifestyle revisited briefly in Summer 1 enquiries.

## Autumn 2: Electrical Circuits & Components

- Circuit symbols, cells, bulbs, buzzers and switches; brightness and volume depend on voltage; fair-test investigations; recording circuits with diagrams.
- Founding year: skipped; basic circuits revised in Summer 1 enquiry weeks.
