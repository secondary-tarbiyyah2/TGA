# Year 9: overview

## At a glance

- Year 9 completes Key Stage 3. It is planned backwards from the Year 10 International GCSE courses, so by July every pupil has met the core skills of each course (English Language B 4EB1, Literature 4ET1, Mathematics A 4MA1, Science Double Award 4SD0, Islamic Studies 4IS1 and the four options).
- 23 lessons of 50 minutes a week: English 4, Maths 4, Science 3, History 1, Geography 1, Computing 1, Art and design 1, Qur'an and Tajweed 3, Arabic 2, Islamic Studies 2, PSHE 1. No PE.
- Founding year: Spring 1 to Summer 2 only (induction week Mon 4 to Fri 8 Jan 2027 for accounts, device checks, meeting teachers and entry assessments, with no live lessons; first live lessons Mon 11 Jan 2027; 94 teaching days over 20 weeks, with 5 in Spring 1). Autumn units are not taught live; the gaps are closed by the bridge (maths and English) and by a three-week review at the start of Autumn 2027.
- Maths and English use the first two weeks (11 and 18 Jan) as a bridge for missed Autumn essentials. Every other subject starts its Spring unit on 11 Jan. Spring 1 has five dated weeks, so each subject drops one Spring 1 week (the X week where there is one).
- PSHE in Spring 2 includes choosing Year 10 options: two of Arabic, Business, Computer Science and Geography (2 lessons each a week in Years 10 and 11).
- Every plan week is tagged E (essential) or X (extension). In the founding year Summer 1 has 5 dated weeks and Summer 2 has 4, so X weeks are the ones dropped; E weeks are always taught.
- Fieldwork (geography) and practicals (science) are done at home or locally with household items, a written guide and a live debrief. No trips, visitors or theme weeks.
- Portal lessons already exist for: Science 9E Making materials (y9 lessons 9 to 16), Geography Resources, energy and sustainability (lessons 1 to 8), PSHE money and careers (lessons 3 and 4), parts of Islamic Studies, Arabic and Qur'an. Most other Spring and Summer weeks below still need lessons writing.

## Assessment

- Baseline on entry: induction week (Mon 4 to Fri 8 Jan): GL entry assessments, reading age, Qur'an recitation placement, Arabic placement. Late joiners sit the same on arrival.
- Every lesson: retrieval starter and exit ticket on the big question. End of every unit: 15 to 20 minute portal quiz in secure test mode (not PSHE).
- End of each half term: hifdh check and tajweed recitation against the rubric (Qur'an groups). Half-term ends: Spring 1 on Fri 12 Feb, Spring 2 on Fri 23 Apr, Summer 1 on Fri 11 Jun, Summer 2 on Fri 9 Jul.
- Assessment point 2 (founding year): late April, core subject tests (week of 26 Apr); report to parents. There is no point 1 in the founding year.
- Assessment point 3: end-of-year tests in every subject in June (TO CONFIRM: exact week, planned for weeks of 21 and 28 Jun); report to parents in July.
- Teachers record one judgement per subject at each point: working towards, at or above the expected standard for the year. Pupils working towards get a support plan before the next point.
- Year 9 has no mock papers. The first mocks come in June of Year 10.

# English

## At a glance

- 4 lessons a week. Planned backwards from International GCSE English Language B (4EB1, one 3-hour paper) and English Literature (4ET1).
- Year shape: Of Mice and Men (Autumn), non-fiction reading then directed writing (Spring), unseen poetry then Julius Caesar (Summer).
- Founding year: Autumn is replaced by a two-week bridge (11 and 18 Jan) of the essentials: Of Mice and Men chapters 3 to 6 and the whole-text essay. Portal english y9 lessons 1 to 8 cover chapters 1 and 2 and essay planning only.
- TO CONFIRM: text suitability for Of Mice and Men (racist language, violence, a mercy killing) and the set non-fiction and poems; the teacher vets every unseen poem and article before use (no music or romance themes).
- Spring and Summer have no portal lessons yet: all 4 lessons a week need writing, using the exam-style passages and the English workbooks as models.

## Autumn 1: Of Mice and Men: novel study

- Founding year: not taught live; the essentials are covered in the bridge (11 Jan week).
- Normal year: America in the 1930s and the American Dream, Burns's To a Mouse, then chapters 1 to 6 (setting, character, theme, structure).
- Portal english y9 lessons 1 to 7 cover context and chapters 1 and 2; chapters 3 to 6 still to write.
- Revisited in the review at the start of Autumn 2027.

## Autumn 2: Of Mice and Men: the extended response

- Founding year: the whole-text essay is taught in the bridge (18 Jan week), then used as the model for later writing.
- Normal year: whole-text essay on character and theme, planning with PEE/PEAL paragraphs, timed practice, redrafting.
- Portal english y9 lesson 8 (writing the response) is the bridge to 4ET1 modern prose.
- Skipped in full in the founding year beyond the bridge; revisited in the Autumn 2027 review.

# Maths

## At a glance

- 4 lessons a week, following White Rose Secondary (block names only). Builds towards International GCSE Mathematics A (4MA1, Higher). Resources come from the school's KS3 scheme, Pearson's generic KS3 Maths Progress (Second Edition), Year 9 book ('Year 3' in the scheme), matched to each block.
- From the school scheme and Maths intent: Year 9 ends with an introduction to trigonometry (Comparing shapes: the tangent, sine and cosine ratios), so a Summer 1 extension row teaches it before 4MA1.
- Autumn blocks (straight-line graphs; forming and solving equations; testing conjectures; 3D shapes; constructions and congruence) are not taught live in the founding year. The 11 and 18 Jan bridge covers straight-line graphs and forming and solving equations; the rest are revisited in the Autumn 2027 review.
- The portal maths y9 lessons (Maths Progress indices, standard form, algebra) are used as extra practice for Spring 1 Number and the bridge.
- Calculator and non-calculator practice alternates; exit tickets use 4MA1 style questions. Maths and money keeps the school intent's aim of managing personal finances.

## Autumn 1: Straight-line graphs; forming and solving equations

- Gradient and y = mx + c; parallel lines; forming equations from words and shapes; equations with brackets and unknowns on both sides.
- Resources: Pearson KS3 Maths Progress (Second Edition), the school's KS3 scheme: Year 8 Unit 9 Straight-line graphs (9.2, 9.3); Year 9 8.1 Using y = mx + c, 8.2 More straight-line graphs, 2.1 Solving equations, 6.4 Solving equations. Portal maths y9 lessons 8 to 11.

## Autumn 2: Testing conjectures; 3D shapes; constructions and congruence

- Counterexamples and proof by example; volume and surface area of prisms and cylinders; constructions with compasses; scale diagrams; congruent triangles.
