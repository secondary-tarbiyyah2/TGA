# Year 7: overview

## At a glance
- Year 7 is the first year of Key Stage 3 at TGA: 23 live lessons a week of 50 minutes on Microsoft Teams, maximum 15 per class. English 4, Maths 4, Science 3, History 1, Geography 1, Computing 1, Art and design 1, Qur'an and Tajweed 3, Arabic 2, Islamic Studies 2, PSHE 1.
- The founding year teaches Spring 1 to Summer 2 only: 21 teaching weeks from Mon 4 Jan 2027 (induction day Fri 1 Jan) to Fri 9 Jul 2027. Autumn units are skipped or caught up, then reviewed in a three-week review at the start of Autumn 2027.
- Weeks of 4 Jan and 11 Jan are the maths and English bridge (missed Autumn essentials, chosen from the entry baseline). Every other subject starts its Spring 1 unit in the week of 4 Jan.
- Spring 1 to Summer 2 weeks are tagged ESSENTIAL (ESS) or EXTENSION (EXT) in each subject table. Short half terms in the founding year drop EXT weeks: Summer 1 loses row 6, Summer 2 loses rows 5 and 6; maths and English Spring 1 lose two rows each to the bridge.
- Plan units follow the TGA Curriculum Plan 2027 (the plan wins over the 2026-27 Secondary School booklet). Maths follows White Rose Secondary (block names only). Qur'an hifdh runs 'Abasa, An-Nazi'at, An-Naba' (80 to 78) so Juz' 'Amma is complete by the end of Year 7.
- The portal already holds Year 7 lessons for only some units (English The Explorer 8, Maths 16 (Pearson, not White Rose), Science 16, History 4, Geography 8, Computing 4 (Scratch), Art 2, PSHE 5, Islamic Studies 12, Arabic 8, Qur'an 12). Everything else is to be written; each subject section says which.
- Practicals, fieldwork and art are done at home or locally with household items, a written guide and a live debrief. No trips, visitors or theme weeks. No PE.
- Year 7 is planned backwards from the Year 10 International GCSE courses: constructions and proof (maths), poetry and Shakespeare (English Literature), OS maps and rivers (Geography), algorithms and Python readiness (Computer Science), hifdh and tajweed to the Juz' Tabarak target by Year 9.

## Assessment
- Baseline on entry: induction day (Fri 1 Jan) and week of 4 Jan: GL entry assessments in English and maths, reading age, Qur'an recitation placement (sets the groups), Arabic placement. The baseline decides which bridge content each pupil needs.
- Every lesson: retrieval starter and exit ticket on the big question. End of every unit: short portal quiz of 15 to 20 minutes in secure test mode (not PSHE).
- End of each half term (founding year): S1 quiz week of 8 Feb; S2 quiz week of 19 Apr; Su1 quiz week of 7 Jun; Su2 is covered by the end-of-year tests.
- Qur'an: hifdh check and tajweed recitation against the rubric at the end of every half term (8 Feb, 19 Apr, 7 Jun, 28 Jun). Daily home revision of 10 to 15 minutes logged in the portal.
- Assessment point 2 (founding year: late April): core subject tests in English, Maths and Science in the week of 26 Apr; report to parents.
- Assessment point 3 (June): end-of-year tests: English, Maths, Science in the week of 21 Jun; all other subjects in the week of 28 Jun; report to parents in July (week of 5 Jul).
- Teachers record one judgement per subject at each point: working towards, at or above the expected standard. Pupils working towards get a support plan before the next point.
- No Year 7 mocks. Autumn gaps are checked again in the Autumn 2027 three-week review.

# English
## At a glance
- 4 lessons a week. Year 7 builds reading, descriptive and narrative writing, poetry analysis and first Shakespeare, preparing for International GCSE English Language B (4EB1) and English Literature (4ET1).
- School frameworks used throughout: VANCAPP (word classes), STASS (setting), PETZAAL (analytical paragraphs), SOSCHAAMPS (persuasive devices).
- Autumn is the novel The Explorer and narrative writing; Spring is poetry then descriptive writing; Summer is The Tempest (extracts) then speech writing.
- Tags: ESS = essential, EXT = extension (dropped first when time is short).

## Autumn 1: The Explorer: novel study*
- Class novel read in chapters from the class copy (in copyright: never reproduce text; use teacher-written practice extracts).
- Portal: english y7 lessons 1 to 8 (openings, VANCAPP, setting and STASS, character and leadership, non-fiction on the Amazon, turning points, assessment).
- Skills: inference, retrieving evidence, word classes, writing a setting.
- Founding year: skipped; the essentials (VANCAPP, STASS, punctuation) are revisited in the bridge, weeks of 4 and 11 Jan.

## Autumn 2: Narrative writing
- Planning and structuring a narrative: openings, tension, turning point, ending; show-not-tell; dialogue punctuation; editing and redrafting.
- Assessed narrative piece at the end of the half term.
- Founding year: covered in the bridge (week of 11 Jan: setting, structure, a short narrative), then revisited in the Autumn 2027 review.

# Islamic Studies
## At a glance
- 2 lessons a week. Year 7: aqeedah, the Makkan period, hadith and the Sunnah, fiqh of worship, Important Lessons for Every Muslim (units 1, 4, 5).
- Portal islamic-studies y7 lessons 1 to 12 exist. Authentic sources only; never invent hadith, ayat or scholars' words. Prophets are never shown in images.
- Prepares for International GCSE Islamic Studies (4IS1).

## Autumn 1: Foundations of aqeedah
- Tawhid and its three categories; shirk; Allah's names and attributes (portal islamic-studies y7 lessons 1 to 3).
- Founding year: skipped; taught in Summer 2 as catch-up.

## Autumn 2: The Makkan period
- Hira and the first revelation; the call; persecution and patience; migration to Abyssinia (portal lessons 4 to 7).
- Founding year: skipped; taught in Summer 2 as catch-up.
