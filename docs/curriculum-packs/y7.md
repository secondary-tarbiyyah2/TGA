# Year 7: overview

## At a glance
- Year 7 is the first year of Key Stage 3 at TGA: 23 live lessons a week of 50 minutes on Microsoft Teams, maximum 15 per class. English 4, Maths 4, Science 3, History 1, Geography 1, Computing 1, Art and design 1, Qur'an and Tajweed 3, Arabic 2, Islamic Studies 2, PSHE 1.
- The founding year teaches Spring 1 to Summer 2 only: 20 teaching weeks from Mon 11 Jan 2027 to Fri 9 Jul 2027, after induction week Mon 4 to Fri 8 Jan (accounts, device checks, meet teachers, entry and baseline assessments; no live lessons). Fri 1 Jan is a bank holiday. Autumn units are skipped or caught up, then reviewed in a three-week review at the start of Autumn 2027.
- Weeks of 11 Jan and 18 Jan are the maths and English bridge (missed Autumn essentials, chosen from the entry baseline). Every other subject starts its Spring 1 unit in the week of 11 Jan.
- Spring 1 to Summer 2 weeks are tagged ESSENTIAL (ESS) or EXTENSION (EXT) in each subject table. Short half terms in the founding year drop EXT weeks: Summer 1 loses row 6, Summer 2 loses rows 5 and 6; Spring 1 has five dated weeks, so maths and English Spring 1 lose two rows to the bridge and one more row each, and every other subject drops one Spring 1 row.
- Plan units follow the TGA Curriculum Plan 2027, except English, History and Geography, which follow the school's own 2026-27 schemes of work and subject intents (November 2026 alignment). Maths follows White Rose Secondary (block names only). Qur'an hifdh runs 'Abasa, An-Nazi'at, An-Naba' (80 to 78) so Juz' 'Amma is complete by the end of Year 7.
- The portal already holds Year 7 lessons for only some units (English The Explorer 8, Maths 16 (Pearson, not White Rose), Science 16, History 4, Geography 8, Computing 4 (Scratch), Art 2, PSHE 5, Islamic Studies 12, Arabic 8, Qur'an 12). Everything else is to be written; each subject section says which.
- Practicals, fieldwork and art are done at home or locally with household items, a written guide and a live debrief. No trips, visitors or theme weeks. No PE.
- Year 7 is planned backwards from the Year 10 International GCSE courses: constructions and proof (maths), poetry and Shakespeare (English Literature), OS maps and rivers (Geography), algorithms and Python readiness (Computer Science), hifdh and tajweed to the Juz' Tabarak target by Year 9.

## Assessment
- Baseline on entry: induction week (Mon 4 to Fri 8 Jan): GL entry assessments in English and maths, reading age, Qur'an recitation placement (sets the groups), Arabic placement. The baseline decides which bridge content each pupil needs.
- Every lesson: retrieval starter and exit ticket on the big question. End of every unit: short portal quiz of 15 to 20 minutes in secure test mode (not PSHE).
- End of each half term (founding year): S1 quiz week of 8 Feb; S2 quiz week of 19 Apr; Su1 quiz week of 7 Jun; Su2 is covered by the end-of-year tests.
- Qur'an: hifdh check and tajweed recitation against the rubric at the end of every half term (8 Feb, 19 Apr, 7 Jun, 28 Jun). Daily home revision of 10 to 15 minutes logged in the portal.
- Assessment point 2 (founding year: late April): core subject tests in English, Maths and Science in the week of 26 Apr; report to parents.
- Assessment point 3 (June): end-of-year tests: English, Maths, Science in the week of 21 Jun; all other subjects in the week of 28 Jun; report to parents in July (week of 5 Jul).
- Teachers record one judgement per subject at each point: working towards, at or above the expected standard. Pupils working towards get a support plan before the next point.
- No Year 7 mocks. Autumn gaps are checked again in the Autumn 2027 three-week review.

# English
## At a glance
- 4 lessons a week in the school's weekly sequence: 1 Literature, 2 Non-fiction, 3 Language (grammar, vocabulary and writing), 4 Assessment, reflection and improvement (Micro-DIRT).
- Concept: Transformation and the Individual (how do people change through experience?). Texts in order: The Explorer, Coraline, Holes, The Tempest, identity and belonging poems, then biographies.
- Frameworks: VANCAPPI, PETZAAL, PESTAAL, STASS, APPS, TSSS, PEEDL, GSP (English Language and Literature Intent 2026-27). Weekly retrieval and reading journal; half-termly assessments.
- The school's Ramadan Literacy Programme (HT4) is taught in Summer 2 because TGA has no lessons in Ramadan.

Resources: Order, texts and assessments: Tarbiyyah Secondary English Language and Literature weekly scheme of work 2026-27 (Year 7 HT1, 7 weeks) and the English Language and Literature Intent 2026-27. # Mathematics
## At a glance
- 4 lessons a week. Block order follows White Rose Secondary (block names only). The school's own KS3 maths scheme is Pearson's generic KS3 Maths Progress (Second Edition) Core Curriculum scheme of work: its lessons, Check, Strengthen and Extend lessons and End of Unit tests are the resources named on each unit below, matched to the White Rose blocks.
- The school scheme starts Year 7 with Analysing and displaying data, ratio and transformations; in the White Rose order these come in Year 8 (data, ratio) and Years 8 and 9 (reflection, rotation, translation), so nothing in the scheme is lost.
- Year 7 prepares for Year 10 International GCSE Mathematics A (4MA1, Higher): number, algebra, geometry and probability foundations.
- Founding year: bridge in the weeks of 11 and 18 Jan from the entry baseline (GL, taken in induction week), then Spring 1 content in three weeks. The portal's 16 Year 7 maths lessons are Maths Progress lessons (data and number skills): reused where the block matches.
- Equipment needed at home from week 1 of Summer 1: ruler, protractor, pair of compasses, squared paper.

- Number and shape sequences; term-to-term rules; the language of algebra: function machines and expressions; forming and simplifying simple expressions; substitution; equal and equivalent; the = and ≡ signs.

- Founding year: not taught as a unit in 2027: essentials in the bridge week of 18 Jan; full block in the Autumn 2027 review.
