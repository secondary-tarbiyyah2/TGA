The school day

Structured like a school, flexible like home.

Live, timetabled lessons through the day, woven together with daily Islamic learning, protected time for prayer, and recorded lessons to revisit any time. A real school rhythm — reached from home.

Morning

Live core lessons

The day opens with a short reminder, then live, teacher-led lessons in the core British-curriculum subjects.

Midday

Break & ṣalāh

Time to step away from the screen — a proper break, and protected time for prayer built into the day.

Afternoon

Lessons & independent work

Further live lessons alongside guided independent work, with teachers on hand and homework set through the portal.

Any time

Recorded lessons

Every live lesson is recorded, so a pupil can catch up after a missed day, revise before an exam, or learn across time zones.

A full timetable — with exact lesson times and options for different time zones — is confirmed with families before term begins.

How online school works

A real classroom — reached through a screen, not replaced by one.

Pupils log in to a live, timetabled lesson with their teacher and classmates. They speak, ask, answer and take part — just as they would in a classroom. There is a register, there is homework, and there is a teacher who notices when a child is quiet.

01

Join the live lesson

A few minutes before class, pupils sign in to the virtual classroom — video, audio, whiteboard and chat, all in one place.

02

Take part, in real time

Pupils answer by voice or chat, work at the board, and ask the teacher directly. Lessons are taught, not pre-recorded.

03

Set work & feedback

Homework is set and marked through the portal, with regular feedback so progress is tracked all year — not left to one exam.

04

Review the recording

Every lesson is recorded, so a pupil can revisit a topic, catch up after a missed day, or revise before an exam.
