## 1. About this Policy

The Academy's fees are set by the Senior Leadership Team with the approval of the Trustees. Please read this policy carefully: **acceptance of a place at the Academy is acceptance of the terms and conditions in this policy.**

## 2. Tuition Fees 2026–2027 (January to July 2027)

Live lessons begin on Monday 11 January 2027. The annual figure below is the total tuition for the founding-cohort period, January to July 2027 (seven months), with no charge for December. All fees are inclusive of VAT.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Year 3 – Year 6 | £2,130.00 | £2,087.40 | £1,065.00 | £304.29 |
| Year 7 – Year 9 | £2,796.00 | £2,740.08 | £1,398.00 | £399.43 |

The two payments are due on Friday 8 January 2027 and Sunday 28 March 2027. Monthly instalments are spread across seven payments: the first on Friday 8 January 2027, then the 28th of each month from 28 January to 28 June 2027. Instalments always add up exactly to the annual fee; where rounding makes them uneven, the final instalment absorbs the difference.

## 5. Ways to Pay

- **Standing order**, preferred for monthly plans (form in Appendix A).
- **Card payment**, through the Academy finance team or the Parent Portal.
- **Bank transfer**, to the Academy's account in the name of An Noor Islamic School. The account details are sent with your offer letter. Please use your child's full name as the payment reference.

## 6. Late Payment

The Academy depends on fees being paid on time. If a payment is missed, the fee-payer is given 14 days' written notice to pay. If payment is not made, the pupil's access to lessons is suspended and the place is held for a further 14 days. If payment is still not received, the place is released to the waiting list. Families experiencing difficulty should contact the finance team at globalacademy@tarbiyyah.co.uk as soon as possible so that a plan can be agreed. The Academy will not end a pupil's place for unpaid fees until the process above has been completed and the Academy knows where the pupil's education will continue.

## 7. Withdrawing a Pupil

One half term's written notice is required to withdraw a pupil, and fees remain payable for the notice period. Notice is given in writing to globalacademy@tarbiyyah.co.uk, stating where the pupil will continue their education.

## 8. Terms and Conditions

1. Fees cover tuition and core digital learning materials, including lesson resources, worksheets and assessments on the Academy's platforms. The Academy gives at least one half term's notice before adding any item to the costs not covered by fees.
2. Families provide a suitable device, reliable internet, a quiet study space and a way to write on worksheets and the lesson PDF (a tablet with a stylus is recommended; without one, access to printing is required, at home, at a local library or similar), to the published minimum specification. The Academy is not responsible for lessons missed because of a family's equipment or connection.
3. Examination costs are not included. Pupils are entered for International GCSE examinations through Tarbiyyah Secondary School as the examination centre, or through another centre arranged for pupils outside the UK, and the centre's entry fees are billed separately.
4. Extra-curricular activities or additional support with a charge are agreed with parents in writing in advance. No such charge is applied without prior written agreement.
5. Each fee-payer is liable for the full fees and any agreed supplementary charges, unless the Academy has agreed otherwise in writing.
6. Fees fall due on the dates above and are paid in full by standing order, card or bank transfer.
7. Agreed supplementary charges are paid within 14 days of the invoice.
8. Cancellation: because enrolment is agreed at a distance, families may cancel within 14 days of accepting a place without giving a reason. All sums paid are refunded, except that where the family asked for lessons to start within those 14 days, a proportionate charge for lessons delivered may be kept. The application fee is not refundable.
9. Outside the cancellation period, one half term's written notice is required to withdraw, and fees are payable for the notice period.
10. Late payment is managed as described above. Reinstatement after a place has been released is at the Academy's discretion and incurs a £50 administration charge in addition to overdue fees.
11. The Academy may suspend access to lessons while fees remain unpaid and may decline to provide references while an account is in arrears.
12. Fees are not normally reduced because of absence.
13. The Academy delivers tuition with reasonable care and skill. Where the Academy cancels a lesson, it will reschedule it, provide an equivalent recorded or supervised session, or credit a proportionate amount. Nothing in this policy affects families' statutory rights.
14. Fees are reviewed each year. At least one half term's written notice of any increase is given. Families may withdraw without penalty or notice within 21 days of being told of an increase that takes effect during a period for which they have accepted a place, and any fees paid in advance for later periods will be refunded.
15. The Academy will seek to recover outstanding fees. Where an account is referred to a collection agency or the courts, reasonable costs actually incurred may be claimed.
16. Where a pupil is withdrawn without the required notice, an administration charge of £210 applies, reflecting account closure, transfer of records and loss of the place at short notice. It does not apply within the cancellation period, after a fee increase notice, where proper notice was given, or where the Academy ended the placement.
17. The application fee of £150 per child is non-refundable.
18. A pupil joining after Monday 11 January 2027 is charged from the start of the half term in which they join. Where a pupil starts after the start of the second half term, an additional £180 administration charge applies, covering the extra planning and assessment needed.
