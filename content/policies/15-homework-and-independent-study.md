## 1. Purpose

Homework and independent study consolidate learning, build habits of self-discipline and prepare pupils for lessons and examinations. Because our pupils already learn through a screen, we design homework to be purposeful and, where possible, completed away from the screen.

## 2. Principles

- Homework is purposeful, linked to the curriculum and explained clearly.
- It is set and submitted through the Student Portal, with deadlines visible on the Student Portal.
- A balance of on-screen and off-screen tasks, including reading, written work in exercise books and memorisation.
- Tasks are achievable for all pupils, with adjustments for SEND.

## 3. Expected Time

| Year group | Typical time per weekday |
| --- | --- |
| Years 1–2 | 10–20 minutes: reading with an adult, phonics, number games, Qur'an practice |
| Years 3–6 | 20–40 minutes: reading, spelling, times tables, one subject task, Qur'an |
| Years 7–9 | 45–75 minutes: tasks set on a published homework timetable |
| Years 10–11 | 1.5–2 hours: examination practice and revision |

A homework timetable for each year group is published on the Student Portal at the start of each term, so that pupils do not receive several large tasks on the same evening.

## 4. Roles

- Teachers set homework on the Student Portal with clear instructions and deadlines, and give feedback in line with the Marking and Feedback Policy.
- Pupils complete work independently and honestly and submit on time, asking for help early if needed.
- Parents provide a quiet space, check the Parent Portal regularly, and encourage good habits without doing the work.

## 5. Late or Missing Homework

Missing homework is followed up by the teacher. Repeated non-completion is managed under the Behaviour and Online Conduct Policy (C2–C3), with support such as a supervised homework club where barriers are identified.

## 6. Monitoring and Review

This policy is reviewed annually, taking account of pupil wellbeing and parent feedback.
