## 1. Our Promise

Strong partnership between home and school is the foundation of a pupil's success, especially online. This charter sets out how the Academy communicates with families, what you can expect from us, and what we ask of you.

## 2. Channels

| Channel | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Parent Portal | Your child's timetable, attendance, merits, reports, documents, invoices, booking meetings, reporting absence, messages to staff |
| Academy email | Formal letters, policies, newsletters and important notices |
| Phone / text | Urgent matters, same-day absence follow-up and emergencies |
| Video meetings | Parent–teacher consultations, pastoral meetings and concerns, booked through the Parent Portal |
| Newsletter | A regular newsletter with news, dates and celebration of achievements |

The Academy does not use group chats or social media for communication about individual pupils. General announcements may also be shared through a one-way Academy broadcast channel, in which parents cannot post.

## 3. Response Times

| Type of message | We aim to respond within |
| --- | --- |
| Safeguarding concern | Immediately — contact the DSL; in an emergency call local emergency services |
| Absence or urgent welfare matter | The same school day |
| General message to a teacher or form tutor | Two school days (48 hours) |
| Concern needing investigation | Acknowledged within two school days; resolved or updated within five |
| Formal complaint | As set out in the Concerns and Complaints Policy |

Staff respond during the Academy working day (UK time). We do not expect staff to reply in the evenings, at weekends or in holidays, except for safeguarding.

## 4. What We Ask of Parents

- Use the Parent Portal or Academy email for messages, rather than contacting staff personally.
- Keep contact details, address and emergency contacts up to date.
- Read Academy communications and respond to requests on time.
- Raise concerns early and with the right person, following the steps in the Concerns and Complaints Policy.
- Communicate courteously. The Academy will not tolerate abusive or threatening communication towards staff.

## 5. Keeping You Informed

| What | When |
| --- | --- |
| Attendance alerts | The same day for any unexplained absence |
| Merits and behaviour notes | Visible on the Parent Portal as they happen |
| Progress reports | Each term |
| Parent–teacher consultations | At least twice a year |
| Newsletter | Every half term, with a short weekly update email |
| Parent information sessions | Each term, on topics such as online safety, exams and wellbeing |

## 6. How Families Shape the Academy

- An annual parent survey, with results and actions shared.
- Short feedback forms after events and information sessions.
- A Parent Forum that meets online each term with the Headteacher.
- Consultation on key policies, including PSHE.
- A "You said, we did" summary published each year.

Pupils' views are gathered through the Student Council and pupil surveys.

## 7. Review

This charter is reviewed annually with parent feedback.
