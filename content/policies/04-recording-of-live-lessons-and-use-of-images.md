## 1. Purpose

In a physical school, safeguarding is supported by open classrooms, corridors and, in some schools, CCTV. In an online school, the equivalent protection is the recording of live lessons. This policy explains why the Academy records lessons, the lawful basis for doing so, who may access recordings, and how long they are kept. It also covers photographs and video of pupils used for other purposes.

## 2. What is Recorded

- All live lessons, form times, assemblies, intervention sessions and clubs held on the Academy's live-lesson platform (currently Microsoft Teams), joined from the Student Portal.
- All one-to-one sessions between a member of staff and a pupil, unless the DSL has authorised otherwise for a specific safeguarding reason, which is recorded.
- Lesson chat, which is retained by the platform. Parents' meetings are not routinely recorded; if a recording is needed, all participants are told at the start.

## 3. Why Lessons are Recorded

| Purpose | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Safeguarding | To protect pupils and staff, provide evidence if a concern or allegation arises, and deter inappropriate behaviour |
| Behaviour and complaints | To establish facts when an incident or complaint needs investigating |
| Quality assurance | To support senior leaders in monitoring teaching and planning staff development |
| Learning support | Where agreed, to allow a pupil who missed a lesson through illness or an approved reason to catch up, or as a reasonable adjustment for a pupil with SEND |

## 4. Lawful Basis

The Academy processes lesson recordings under Article 6(1)(e) of the UK GDPR (a task carried out in the public interest — the provision of education and safeguarding of children) and, where applicable, Article 6(1)(f) (legitimate interests). Where recordings may reveal special category data, the Academy relies on the substantial public interest condition for safeguarding of children, under Schedule 1 of the Data Protection Act 2018. Families are told about recording in the privacy notice on the Academy website and at enrolment, and a notice appears at the start of every recorded lesson.

## 5. Who Can Access Recordings

| Person | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Designated Safeguarding Lead and deputies | Any recording, for safeguarding purposes |
| Headteacher and senior leaders | For safeguarding, behaviour, complaints or quality assurance |
| Data Protection Officer | To manage requests and retention |
| The teacher who led the lesson | Their own lessons, for reflection and planning |
| Pupils | Only where a recording has been deliberately shared as a learning resource, with other pupils' images removed or limited where appropriate |
| Parents | Not routinely. Parents may make a subject access request for recordings of their own child; other pupils' data will be redacted or withheld as the law requires |
| External agencies | Police, children's social care or the local authority, where required for a safeguarding matter or by law |

Every access to a recording for safeguarding, behaviour or complaint purposes is logged.

## 6. Retention

| Recording type | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Routine lesson recordings | Deleted after one academic year from the date of recording |
| Recordings shared as learning resources | Until the end of the academic year in which they are shared |
| Recordings relevant to a safeguarding concern, allegation, complaint or legal claim | Kept securely until the matter is resolved and then in line with the retention period for that record |

Deletion is carried out by the Digital Lead and confirmed to the Data Protection Officer.

## 7. Pupils and Parents Must Not Record

Pupils and parents must not record, photograph, screenshot or livestream any lesson, member of staff or pupil. Doing so breaches the privacy of others and is managed under the Behaviour and Online Conduct Policy. Posting such material online is treated as a serious breach and may be a safeguarding matter.

## 8. Photographs and Video for Other Purposes

The Academy may wish to use images or video of pupils in the prospectus, on the website, on social media or in celebration events. We will:

- seek separate, specific, written consent from parents (and from pupils aged 13 and over) for each type of use;
- never publish a pupil's full name alongside their image;
- never use images of pupils who are looked-after, adopted or subject to a safeguarding concern without the DSL's agreement;
- allow consent to be withdrawn at any time, after which the image will not be used in new materials. Lesson recordings are never used for marketing.

## 9. Monitoring and Review

This policy is reviewed annually by the Data Protection Officer and DSL and approved by the Headteacher. It is also reviewed if the Academy changes platform.
