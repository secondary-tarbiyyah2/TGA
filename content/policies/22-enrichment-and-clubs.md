## 1. Purpose

Pupils in an online school need rich opportunities to build friendships, develop talents and feel part of a community. Our enrichment programme offers clubs, competitions and events that complement the curriculum and bring the Academy family together.

## 2. Our Offer

| Strand | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Faith and character | Hifdh circles, public speaking, charity projects, Ramadan programme |
| Academic | Maths challenge, science club, debating, creative writing, coding |
| Creative | Art and design, calligraphy, photography (without images of people) |
| Active | Step and activity challenges families can do at home |
| Leadership and service | Student Council, House captains, peer mentoring, community and environmental projects |
| Houses | Inter-House competitions and celebrations across year groups |

The club programme for each term is published on the Student Portal before the term begins.

## 3. How Clubs are Run

- Clubs are run on Academy platforms by Academy staff or approved, DBS-checked volunteers, and follow the Live Lesson Protocols, including recording.
- External providers and guest speakers are approved through the risk assessment in the Safeguarding: Extremism and Radicalisation Policy.
- Pupils sign up through the Student Portal; parents can see their child's clubs on the Parent Portal.
- Clubs are timed to suit different time zones where possible.
- Any in-person event or trip has its own risk assessment and parental consent.

## 4. Charges

Most clubs are free. Any club or activity with a charge is agreed with parents in advance, in line with the Fees Policy.

## 5. Inclusion

Clubs are open to all pupils, with reasonable adjustments for pupils with SEND.

## 6. Monitoring and Review

Participation is tracked to ensure every pupil has the opportunity to take part. The programme is reviewed each term using pupil feedback, and this policy is reviewed annually.
