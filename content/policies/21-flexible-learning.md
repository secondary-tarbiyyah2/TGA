## 1. Purpose

Our pupils live in many countries. For some families, the standard UK timetable fits well; for others, the time difference or family circumstances make it difficult. This policy explains the learning options available, who is eligible, and how quality and safeguarding are maintained.

## 2. Learning Options

| Option | What it involves | Who it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard live timetable | All lessons live, 09:00–15:00 UK time Monday to Thursday and 09:00–12:00 on Fridays (the afternoon is free for Jumu'ah) | Most pupils in the UK and nearby time zones |
| Four-day live week | Live lessons Monday to Thursday; Friday is a structured independent study day with set work, submitted through the Student Portal | Year 1 to Year 9. Not offered in Years 10–11, where GCSE courses need the full live timetable |
| Time-zone flexible | A mix of live lessons at feasible local times and recorded lessons with set tasks and live catch-up clinics | Pupils whose local time makes the full live timetable unreasonable |
| Time-zone pods | Where five or more pupils in the same phase live in a similar time zone (for example the Gulf or South-East Asia), the Academy may run their form time, Qur'an group and catch-up clinics at a time that suits them, so they build friendships with pupils who share their day | Clusters of families abroad |
| Temporary adjusted timetable | A reduced or adapted timetable for a limited period | Pupils with medical needs, SEND or exceptional circumstances, agreed with the SENCO or Pastoral Lead |

The standard live timetable remains our expectation wherever it is reasonable, because live teaching gives the strongest learning and the best safeguarding oversight.

## 3. How to Apply

Families request a Flexible Learning option at admission or at any time through the Parent Portal. The Deputy Headteacher considers the request with the form tutor and, where relevant, the SENCO. A written Flexible Learning Plan sets out: which lessons are live and which are recorded; expected weekly engagement; how work is submitted; live check-in times; and the review date.

## 4. Safeguarding and Attendance

Every pupil, whatever their option, has at least one live, camera-on contact with a member of staff every school day, normally form time or a tutor check-in. Engagement with recorded lessons and set work is tracked, and non-engagement is followed up under the Attendance, Punctuality and Children Missing Education Policy in the same way as absence.

## 5. Recorded Lessons

Recorded lessons used for flexible learners are shared through the Student Portal for the pupil's class only and are subject to the Recording of Live Lessons and Use of Images Policy. Pupils must not share recordings.

## 6. Fees

Flexible Learning options are provided within the standard fee unless the Fees Policy states otherwise.

## 7. Review

Each Flexible Learning Plan is reviewed at least termly. The Academy may end a plan if engagement or progress is insufficient, after discussion with parents. This policy is reviewed annually.
