## 1. Purpose

Regular, punctual attendance at live lessons is essential for progress, wellbeing and safety. In an online school, attendance is also our main daily assurance that each child is safe. This policy sets out how attendance is recorded, what we expect of families, and how we respond to absence.

## 2. Legal and Regulatory Context

The Academy has regard to the DfE guidance Working together to improve school attendance, Keeping Children Safe in Education, the Children Missing Education guidance and the OEAS standards. Families living outside England should also be aware of any compulsory education requirements in their country of residence.

## 3. Expectations

| Area | Expectation |
| --- | --- |
| Attendance target | At least 96% attendance for every pupil |
| Punctuality | Logged in and ready at the start time of every lesson |
| Camera | Camera on for registration so the teacher can see the pupil is present and well |
| Term-time holidays | Not permitted. Leave of absence is granted only in exceptional circumstances by the Headteacher, requested in advance |
| Flexible Learning | Pupils on an agreed Flexible Learning plan follow the attendance arrangements in that plan |

## 4. Registration

- A register is taken in the first five minutes of every live lesson using the live-lesson register and the Student Portal.
- A pupil who joins after registration but within the first ten minutes is marked late. After ten minutes, the pupil is marked late for the lesson and the lateness is recorded as a concern.
- A pupil who is logged in but not present (no camera, no response to direct questions) after a reasonable check is recorded as present but not engaged, and the form tutor is informed.
- Registers are kept for three years.

## 5. Reporting Absence

Parents should report absence before 09:00 UK time on the first day, through the Parent Portal's absence form, stating the reason and the expected return. For absence lasting more than one day, parents update the Academy daily.

| Code (summary) | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Present | Attended and engaged |
| Late | Joined after registration |
| Authorised absence | Illness, medical appointment that could not be arranged outside lessons, religious observance (for example Eid), or exceptional circumstances approved by the Headteacher |
| Unauthorised absence | No reason given, or a reason not accepted, including term-time holidays |
| Technical absence | Unable to connect despite reasonable efforts; recorded separately and reviewed for patterns |

The Academy does not routinely require a doctor's note for short illnesses but may ask for evidence where absence is frequent or prolonged.

## 6. Following Up Absence

1. If no reason has been given, the Academy contacts parents by message and phone by 10:00 UK time (or within one hour of the first missed lesson for pupils on a different time zone pattern).
2. If parents cannot be reached, emergency contacts are tried.
3. If there is still no contact by the end of the day, or there is any welfare concern, the DSL is informed.
4. Where a pupil is absent without explanation for two consecutive days, the DSL considers a welfare call and further action.
5. Where a pupil leaves and the Academy does not know where their education will continue, the Academy may share its concern with the relevant local authority as good practice. For pupils abroad, the DSL decides on appropriate action under the Child Protection and Safeguarding Policy.

## 7. Improving Attendance

| Stage | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Below 96% | Form tutor discusses attendance with the pupil and parents |
| Below 92% | Attendance Lead meeting with parents; support plan agreed and reviewed after four weeks |
| Below 90% (persistent absence) | Formal attendance meeting; consideration of SEND, health, wellbeing or technical barriers; possible Flexible Learning review |
| Continued concern | Headteacher review; for pupils in England, consideration of local authority involvement; the Academy may review whether the placement remains suitable |

Persistent lateness is managed through the Behaviour and Online Conduct Policy and, where connected to the home environment or connectivity, through practical support.

## 8. Technical Difficulties

Families are responsible for a suitable device and connection (see the Fees Policy). Where a pupil cannot connect, they should: try rejoining once; message the form tutor through the Student Portal; and complete the work set for the lesson on the Student Portal. Repeated technical absence will prompt a conversation about support, which may include a device check.

## 9. Pupils with Medical or Additional Needs

The Academy works with families of pupils with long-term illness, SEND or disability to agree reasonable adjustments, such as a reduced or flexible timetable for a time-limited period. Attendance awards are designed so that pupils with a long-term health condition are not disadvantaged; recognition is given for improvement and effort as well as percentages.

## 10. Withdrawal

A pupil is only withdrawn from the admissions record once the Academy is satisfied it knows where the pupil will continue their education. Where a pupil leaves and the Academy does not know where their education will continue, the Academy may share its concern with the relevant local authority as good practice.

## 11. Monitoring and Review

The Attendance Lead reports attendance weekly to senior leaders and termly to trustees, including patterns by year group, SEND and time zone. This policy is reviewed annually.
