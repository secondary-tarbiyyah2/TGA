Questions, answered

The things parents ask first.

What does it cost?

Fees are kept fair and competitive, with the full school day, recordings and pastoral care included. We share the current fees on enquiry — register your interest and we'll send them with no obligation.

Which year groups can join?

The Academy is opening in stages, from primary through to Sixth Form. Register your interest and tell us your child's year group — we'll let you know when it opens.

What will my child need?

A computer or tablet, a reliable internet connection, and a quiet place to work. Everything else — the classroom, materials and recordings — is provided through the school portal.

Who is the Academy for?

Practising Muslim families in the UK and abroad seeking a values-aligned British education — including expat families and those who travel or move often. All who share our ethos are warmly welcome.

Is it really a full school?

Yes. Live timetabled lessons, qualified UK teachers, a register, homework, feedback and reports — the structure of a school, delivered to your home, and backed by an established trust.
